# Blender Buddy v3.77.0 — animation's seventieth piece: rolling the other way

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release. The
public download still serves v3.20.0.

**Make it roll the other way** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first sixty-nine animation
pieces — Buddy's seventieth animation piece, teaching
rolling the other way, in four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will roll the other way** — one thing that will
   travel back across the ground and turn over the other way.
2. **Remember where it starts, and which way it faces** — a place
   memory and a facing memory together at frame 1, at (2, 0, 1), facing level.
3. **Roll it the other way** —
   the far end remembered at frame 48 at (-2, 0, 1),
   turned +4 radians on Y,
   the travel and the turning paced to each other.
4. **Watch it roll the other way, then save it** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the place and facing memories
   in it.

The teaching point is that direction is part of the roll:
the roll piece's checker used the biggest absolute turn, so it could not tell the two directions apart — this piece's checker reads the signed travel across and the signed turn on Y, so the first way reads travel +4.0 and turn -4.0 while the other way reads travel -4.0 and turn +4.0, reading places
(2, 0, 1) / (-2, 0, 1) at frames
1/48 in the reference build, with (0.0638, 0, 1) turned +1.9362 on Y at frame 24. The checks read the real evaluated place and facing
at the first and last memories: the span must run at least 20 frames,
the signed travel must land between -5.5 and -2.0, the signed turn must land between 2.5 and 5.5, and the
travel per radian must sit between 0.4 and 2.5. A probe on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5
before building showed the other-way reference read span 47, signed travel -4.0, signed turn +4.0, ratio 1.0; a first-way roll read signed travel +4.0 with signed turn -4.0,
a slide the other way read signed turn 0.0 with ratio 99.0, a spin the other way read signed travel 0.0, a small roll the other way read signed travel -1.0 with signed turn +1.0, an out-of-pace roll the other way read signed turn +0.5 with ratio 8.0, and a roll the other way that came back read signed travel 0.0 with signed turn 0.0 — all failing the bar. The probe ran on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 with byte-identical outputs.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a starting
place and facing alone does not prove the roll the other way, a first-way roll does not
prove it, a slide the other way does not prove it, a spin the other way does not prove it, a small roll the other way does not prove it, an out-of-pace roll the other way does not prove it, and a roll the other way that comes back does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 at the final
v3.77.0 source: the flows battery (all seventy-eight
flows plus the anim_otherroll negatives), the interface
battery, and the plain-layer and search batteries all
pass on both versions. Proof picture: demo/anim_otherroll_final.png — the
reference build's red thing at frame 24, mid-roll the other way
at (0.0638, 0, 1) turned +1.9362 on Y.
