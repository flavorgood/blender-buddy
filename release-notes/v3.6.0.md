# Blender Buddy v3.6.0 — sculpting's fourth piece: masks and face sets

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes.

**Keep one part safe** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
first three sculpting pieces — Buddy's fourth sculpting piece,
teaching selective work (masks and face sets) the way
sculptors actually use it, in five steps:

1. **Start with fresh clay** — one plain ball, and the part
   you will protect chosen before the work starts.
2. **Give one area its own face set** — a region with its own
   colour that can be hidden, shown and worked one at a
   time. The tick reads the surface's own per-face set
   numbers: a real second set over a real area.
3. **Mask the part you will protect** — protection painted
   on with the Mask brush: where the mask sits, the brushes
   stop moving the clay. The tick reads the mask store
   itself — a real patch covered, the rest left bare;
   masking everything protects nothing and does not tick.
4. **Work the rest — the mask holds** — big work over the
   whole form, straight across the masked patch. The tick
   reads the protection on the surface: the masked points
   still sit tight together on the original ball while
   everything around them has moved.
5. **Finish, and save the divisions too** — smooth shading
   and the file saved, with the face sets and the mask
   still on the clay in the file.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk: a
plain ball ticks only step 1, a face set alone does not
prove the mask, a mask over everything does not prove the
mask step, and work that drags the masked patch along does
not prove protection.

The Start screen's coverage box now names all four
sculpting pieces and still says plainly that they are first
pieces of the craft, not the whole of it; animation and the
other crafts remain untaught. Multires is the one sculpting
piece still not taught.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0: the flows battery (all seven
flows walked end to end, sculpt_masks included, plus its
four negative cases and the DynTopo and symmetry
negatives), the interface battery (including the coverage
box), and the plain-layer and search batteries. The 4.2.5
binary was not present on the build machine this run, so
the 4.2.5 pass is still owed; the full parallel battery
