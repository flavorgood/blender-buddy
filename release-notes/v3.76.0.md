# Blender Buddy v3.76.0 — animation's sixty-ninth piece: orbiting the other way

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release. The
public download still serves v3.20.0.

**Make it orbit the other way** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first sixty-eight animation
pieces — Buddy's sixty-ninth animation piece, teaching
orbiting the other way, in four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will orbit the other way** — one thing that will
   circle a centre the other way and come home.
2. **Remember its starting place** — a place
   memory at frame 1, out at the edge of the circle, at (3, 0, 1).
3. **Orbit the other way** —
   the quarter points remembered in the other order: (0, -3, 1) at frame 13,
   (-3, 0, 1) at frame 25, (0, 3, 1) at frame 37,
   and home to (3, 0, 1) at frame 48,
   the same distance from the middle the whole way.
4. **Watch it orbit the other way, then save** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the place memories
   in it.

The teaching point is that direction is part of the orbit:
the orbit piece's sweep used the absolute angle, so it could not tell the two directions apart — this piece's checker sums the signed angle, so the first way reads about +6.2832 and the other way about -6.2832, reading places
(3, 0, 1) / (0, -3, 1) / (-3, 0, 1) / (0, 3, 1) / (3, 0, 1) at frames
1/13/25/37/48 in the reference build. The checks sample the real evaluated place
across the span: the span must run at least 20 frames,
the signed sweep must land between -6.6 and -5.0, the mean radius must be at least 1.5, the radius variation must be at most 0.5, and the
ends gap must be at most 0.3. A probe on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5
before building showed the other-way reference read span 47, signed sweep -6.2832, mean radius 2.8673, radius variation 0.2480, ends gap 0.0; a first-way orbit read signed sweep +6.2832,
a quarter arc the other way read span 12 with signed sweep -3.1416 and ends gap 4.2426, a straight move read variation 1.5586 with ends gap 6.0, an out-and-back read signed sweep 0.0 with variation 1.5247, a small other-way circle read mean radius 0.9558, and an ellipse-ish other-way path read variation 1.0027 — all failing the bar. The probe ran on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 with byte-identical outputs.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a starting
place alone does not prove the orbit the other way, a first-way orbit does not
prove it, a quarter arc the other way does not prove it, a straight move does not prove it, an out-and-back does not prove it, a small circle the other way does not prove it, and an ellipse-ish path the other way does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 at the final
v3.76.0 source: the flows battery (all seventy-seven
flows plus the anim_otherorbit negatives), the interface
battery, and the plain-layer and search batteries all
pass on both versions. Proof picture: demo/anim_otherorbit_final.png — the
reference build's red thing at frame 13, a quarter of the way round the other way
at (0, -3, 1).
