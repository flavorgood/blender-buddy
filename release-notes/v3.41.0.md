# Blender Buddy v3.41.0 — animation's thirty-fourth piece: swaying

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release.

**Make it sway** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first thirty-three animation
pieces — Buddy's thirty-fourth animation piece, teaching
swaying, in four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will sway** — one thing that will
   lean from side to side.
2. **Remember its starting facing, upright** — a facing
   memory at frame 1, level on every axis.
3. **Sway it side to side and settle it upright** — leaned
   0.8 radians one way remembered at frame 17, leaned 0.8
   the other way remembered at frame 33, and upright again
   remembered at frame 48, in its own place the whole way.
4. **Watch it sway, then save it** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the facing memories in it.

The teaching point is that a sway is both leans and the
settling together: the tilt leaned a thing one way and
left it there — a sway comes back, reading facings 0.0 /
0.8 / -0.8 / 0.0 at frames 1/17/33/48 in the reference
build. The checks sample the real evaluated facing across
the span: the span must run at least 20 frames, on the
side axis that leans the most the lean must reach at
least +0.6 one way and at least -0.6 the other, the lean
must change direction at least twice, the ends must land
within 0.3 of level, the upright (Z) facing must hold
within 0.35, and the thing must stay within 0.5 of its
place. A probe on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 before building
showed the facing memories level at frame 1, leaned +0.8
on the X axis at frame 17, leaned -0.8 at frame 33 and
level again at frame 48 read back and interpolate
identically on both versions (span 47, most lean +0.8 and
-0.8, reversals 2, ends 0.0, upright drift 0.0, place
drift 0.0 in the probe); a tilt read most lean the other
way 0.0 with reversals 0 and ends 0.8, a spin read
upright drift 6.2832, a small sway read +0.2 and -0.2,
and a one-way rock read most lean the other way 0.0 with
reversals 1 — all failing the bar.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, an upright facing alone
does not prove the sway, a tilt does not prove it, a spin
does not prove it, a small sway does not prove it, and a
one-way rock does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 at the final v3.41.0
source: the flows battery (all forty-two flows plus the
anim_sway negatives), the interface battery, and the
plain-layer and search batteries all pass. Proof picture:
demo/anim_sway_final.png — the reference build's red
thing at frame 17, leaned over to one side at 0.8 radians
on its way through the sway.
