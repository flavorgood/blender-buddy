# Blender Buddy v3.81.0 — animation's seventy-fourth piece: nodding the other way

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release. The
public download still serves v3.20.0.

**Make it nod the other way** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first seventy-three animation
pieces — Buddy's seventy-fourth animation piece, teaching
nodding the other way, in four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will nod the other way** — one thing that will
   lean a little way over the other way, twice, and come back up
   each time.
2. **Remember its starting facing, upright** — a facing
   memory at frame 1, level, rotation (0, 0, 0).
3. **Nod it the other way twice and bring it back upright each time** —
   the leaned facing remembered at frame 13 at -0.5
   on the nod axis, leaned the other way, upright
   again remembered at frame 24, -0.5 again remembered
   at frame 37, upright again remembered at frame 48, the other axes
   staying level and the place held.
4. **Watch it nod the other way, then save it** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the facing memories
   in it.

The teaching point is that direction is part of the nod:
the nod piece's checker read the lean on whichever side axis leaned the most and took the peak by absolute value, so it could not tell the two directions apart — this piece's checker reads the signed lean on the nod axis, so the first way reads peak 0.0 with other-way +0.5 while the other way reads peak -0.5 with other-way 0.0, reading facings
(0, 0, 0) / (-0.5, 0, 0) / (0, 0, 0) / (-0.5, 0, 0) / (0, 0, 0) at frames
1/13/24/37/48 in the reference build. The checks sample the real evaluated facing
across the span: the span must run at least 20 frames,
the peak signed lean must land between -0.7 and -0.35, the lean the first way must stay within 0.15, the lean must change direction at least three times, the ends must land within 0.3 of level, the other axes must hold within 0.35, and the
thing must stay within 0.5 of its place. A probe on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5
before building showed the other-way reference read span 47, peak -0.5, other-way 0.0, reversals 3, ends 0.0, Y drift 0.0, Z drift 0.0, place drift 0.0 (frame 24 reading the keyed upright (0, 0, 0) and frame 36 reading -0.4916, near the bottom of the second nod); a first-way nod read peak 0.0 with other-way 0.5,
a bow the other way read peak -0.9 with reversals 1, a tilt the other way read peak -1.0 with reversals 0 and ends 1.0, a sway read peak -0.8 with other-way 0.8, a spin read Z drift 6.2832, a small nod the other way read peak -0.15, a single nod the other way read peak -0.5 with reversals 1, and a nod the other way that moved read place drift 3.0 — all failing the bar. The probe ran on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 with byte-identical outputs.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a starting
facing alone does not prove the nod the other way, a first-way nod does not
prove it, a bow the other way does not prove it, a tilt the other way does not prove it, a sway does not prove it, a spin does not prove it, a small nod the other way does not prove it, and a single nod the other way does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 at the final
v3.81.0 source: the flows battery (all eighty-two
flows plus the anim_othernod negatives), the interface
battery, and the plain-layer and search batteries all
pass on both versions. Proof picture: demo/anim_othernod_final.png — the
reference build's red thing at frame 13, leaned the other way
at facing (-0.5, 0, 0) in its own place.
