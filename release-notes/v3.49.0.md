# Blender Buddy v3.49.0 — animation's forty-second piece: sidestepping

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release.

**Make it sidestep** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first forty-one animation
pieces — Buddy's forty-second animation piece, teaching
sidestepping, in four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will sidestep** — one thing that will
   step sideways and stay there.
2. **Remember where it starts, on the ground** — a place
   memory at frame 1, its centre no more than 1.3 units up.
3. **Sidestep it sideways and keep it there** —
   moved sideways 2.0 units along Y remembered at frame 24, and
   the same sidestepped place remembered again at frame 48, at
   the same ground height the whole way.
4. **Watch it sidestep, then save** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the place memories
   in it.

The teaching point is that a sidestep is the sideways
step and the staying together: the slide piece sent a thing
travelling forward along the ground, and the pace piece
stepped a thing out sideways and brought it back home — a
sidestep steps sideways, along the Y axis, and stays in the
new place, on the ground the whole way, never travelling
forward and never leaving the ground, reading places
(0, 0, 0) / (0, 2, 0) / (0, 2, 0) at frames
1/24/48 in the reference build (0.9348 sideways partway
across at frame 12). The checks sample the real evaluated place
across the span: the span must run at least 20 frames,
the sideways displacement from the start place to the end
place must land between 1.7 and 2.3, the forward drift
along X must stay within 0.5 across all samples, the end
height must land within 0.3 of the start height, and the
total sampled height range must stay within 0.35. A probe on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5
before building showed the place memories at
(0, 0, 0), (0, 2, 0) and (0, 2, 0) across frames
1/24/48 read back and interpolate identically on both
versions (span 47, sideways 2.0, forward drift
0.0, start 0.0, end 0.0, height range 0.0 in the probe); a slide read sideways 0.0
with forward drift 3.0, a pace read sideways 0.0 ending
home, a small sidestep read sideways 0.5, a diagonal move
read sideways 2.0 with forward drift 2.0, a hop read
height range 1.5, and a rise read sideways 0.0 with end
height 2.0 and height range 2.0 — all failing the bar.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a ground starting
place alone does not prove the sidestep, a slide does not
prove it, a pace does not prove it, a small sidestep does not
prove it, a diagonal move does not prove it, and a hop
does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 at the final
v3.49.0 source: the flows battery (all fifty
flows plus the anim_sidestep negatives), the interface
battery, and the plain-layer and search batteries all
pass. Proof picture: demo/anim_sidestep_final.png — the
reference build's red thing at frame 36, in its
sidestepped place on the ground with its
shadow below.
