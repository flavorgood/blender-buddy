# Blender Buddy v3.73.0 — animation's sixty-sixth piece: spinning the other way

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release. The
public download still serves v3.20.0.

**Make it spin the other way** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first sixty-five animation
pieces — Buddy's sixty-sixth animation piece, teaching
spinning the other way, in four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will spin the other way** — one thing that will
   turn one full turn the other way and face the way it started again.
2. **Remember its starting facing** — a facing
   memory at frame 1, level, rotation (0, 0, 0).
3. **Spin it a full turn the other way and keep the facing it lands on** —
   rotation Z to -3.1416 remembered at frame 24, and
   rotation Z to -6.2832 remembered again at frame 48,
   the side axes staying level and the place held the whole way.
4. **Watch it spin the other way, then save** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the facing memories
   in it.

The teaching point is that the other way is the opposite full turn:
the spin piece spun a thing all the way around one way — this piece spins
it all the way around the other way, reading facings
(0, 0, 0) / (0, 0, -3.1416) / (0, 0, -6.2832) at frames
1/24/48 in the reference build ((0, 0, -5.3145) at frame 36 as well). The checks sample the real evaluated facing
across the span: the span must run at least 20 frames,
the end turn on the upright axis must land between -6.6 and -5.9, the side axes must hold within 0.35, and the
thing must stay within 0.5 of its place. A probe on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5
before building showed the facing memories
level at frame 1, turned -3.1416 on the Z axis at frame 24 and -6.2832 at frame 48 read back (span 47, ends
-6.2832, side drift 0.0, place drift 0.0 in the probe); a spin the first way read ends 6.2832,
a turn around the other way read ends -3.1416, a turn to the other side read ends -1.5708, a wag read ends 0.0, a small turn read ends -0.4, a tilt read ends 0.0 with side drift 1.0, and a spin that returned
read ends 0.0 — all failing the bar. The probe ran on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 with byte-identical outputs.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a starting
facing alone does not prove the spin the other way, a spin the first way does not
prove it, a turn around the other way does not prove it, a small turn does not prove it, a tilt does not prove it, and a spin that returns does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 at the final
v3.73.0 source: the flows battery (all seventy-four
flows plus the anim_otherspin negatives), the interface
battery, and the plain-layer and search batteries all
pass on both versions. Proof picture: demo/anim_otherspin_final.png — the
reference build's red thing at frame 24, halfway around the other way
at facing (0, 0, -3.1416) in its own place (a cube reads the same halfway around, so the numeric proof carries it, as with the turn pieces).
