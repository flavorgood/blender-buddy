# Blender Buddy v3.33.0 — animation's twenty-sixth piece: squashing

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release.

**Make it squash** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first twenty-five animation pieces —
Buddy's twenty-sixth animation piece, teaching squashing, in
four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will squash** — one thing that will
   flatten down and bulge out.
2. **Remember its starting size** — a size memory at
   frame 1, at its own shape.
3. **Squash it flat, then let it settle back** — flattened
   to 0.4 of its height with its sides bulged to 1.5
   remembered at frame 24, its own size remembered again
   at frame 48.
4. **Watch it squash, then save it** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the size memories in it.

The teaching point is that a squash is the flatten-and-bulge
together, with a settle home: the pulse swelled a thing
evenly and the shrink made it smaller on every side, a
squash does neither — reading (1.2337, 1.2337, 0.7196)
partway down at frame 12 and (1.5, 1.5, 0.4) flattened and
bulged at frame 24. The checks sample the real evaluated
size across the span: the span must run at least 20 frames,
the height must fall to at most 0.55 of its start somewhere
across the span, a side must bulge to at least 1.25 of its
start, and the size at the end must land within 0.3 of the
starting size. A probe on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 before
building showed the size memories at (1, 1, 1),
(1.5, 1.5, 0.4) and (1, 1, 1) across frames 1/24/48 read
back and interpolate identically on both versions (span 47,
low height 0.4, bulge 1.5, ends gap 0.0 in the probe); a
shrink read bulge 1.0 with ends 0.6, a pulse read low
height 1.0, a stretch read low height 1.0 with ends 1.5, a
small squash read low height 0.8 with bulge 1.1, and a
squash that stayed squashed read ends 0.6 — all failing
the bar.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a starting size alone
does not prove the squash, a shrink does not prove it, a
pulse does not prove it, and a small squash does not prove
it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 AND 4.2.5 at the final v3.33.0
source: the flows battery (all thirty-four flows plus
the anim_squash negatives), the interface battery, and
the plain-layer and search batteries all pass on both
versions. Proof picture: demo/anim_squash_final.png —
the reference build's red thing at frame 24, flattened
to 0.4 of its height with its sides bulged to 1.5,
resting flat on the ground.
