# Blender Buddy v3.18.0 — animation's eleventh piece: changing colour

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes.

**Make it change colour** sits under "Learn by doing" beside
the modeling, sculpting and first ten animation pieces —
Buddy's eleventh animation piece, teaching changing colour,
in four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will change colour** — one thing
   whose paint will travel.
2. **Remember its starting colour** — a colour memory at
   frame 1 on the paint's Base Color slot, the reference
   build wearing red, holding the colour the change
   travels FROM.
3. **Change the colour, and remember again** — a clearly
   different colour (blue) remembered at frame 48.
4. **Watch it change, then save it** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with both colour memories
   in it.

The teaching point is that the memories can hold a thing's
PAINT as a journey of its own: one colour, a different
colour, and Blender blending between them — red to blue
passes through purple (frame 24 reads 0.451 / 0.183 /
0.477). The colour memories live on the material's node
tree and are read through the same cross-version helper
as every other memory. The checks read the memories
themselves and the real evaluated colour at the first
and last memories: the span must run at least 20 frames
and the colour must really travel — a distance of at
least 0.6 through colour space, where the reference
build's red-to-blue measures about 1.1. A probe on
Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 before building showed a red
memory at frame 1 and a blue one at frame 48 read back
and blend identically on both versions.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a starting colour alone
does not prove the change, and a nudge of the same colour
does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 AND 4.2.5 at the final v3.18.0
source: the flows battery (all nineteen flows plus the
anim_color negatives), the interface battery, and the
plain-layer and search batteries all pass on both
versions. Proof picture: demo/anim_color_final.png —
the reference build's thing at frame 48, fully blue at
the end of its change from red, on the ground with its
shadow below.
