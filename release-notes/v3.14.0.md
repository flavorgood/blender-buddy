# Blender Buddy v3.14.0 — animation's seventh piece: following

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes.

**Bring a second thing along** sits under "Learn by
doing" beside the modeling, sculpting and first six
animation pieces — Buddy's seventh animation piece,
teaching following, in five steps:

1. **Pick the leader** — one thing that will carry
   the move.
2. **Give the leader a move that travels** — its
   place remembered at frame 1, moved a clear
   distance and remembered again at frame 48, the
   first piece's own way.
3. **Bring in a second thing** — a plain shape
   sitting a clear step away from the leader (the
   reference build sets it a step and a half above,
   riding on top), with no memories of its own.
4. **Tie it to the leader** — Blender's parent tie,
   made keeping its place, so the follower stays
   exactly where it sits and is carried from there.
5. **Watch them travel together, then save it** —
   played as a pair, and the file saved with the
   tie still in it.

The teaching point is how groups, riders, loads and
props actually move in animation: not by remembering
every thing's journey separately, but by tying the
followers to one leader whose journey is remembered
once. The checks read the leader's memories
themselves (a span of at least 20 frames, a clear
distance travelled), the tie itself (the follower's
parent must BE the leader), and the pair's real
world places read across the span — the follower
must really travel (the reference build's follower
travels 3.16 units) and the gap between the pair
must hold (the reference gap change is 0.0). A
probe on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 before building
showed a tied follower rides its leader's remembered
move identically on both versions, its gap unchanged
at every frame.

One render was rejected on sight during the build:
with the follower set a step behind the leader, the
proof camera saw only a sliver of it behind the
leader's bulk — the reference follower now rides on
top, and the pair reads clearly in the proof
picture.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the
walk: a plain scene ticks only step 1, a travelling
leader alone does not prove the second thing, a
second thing sitting untied does not prove the tie,
and a pair tied to a leader that never travels does
not prove the finish.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 AND 4.2.5 at the final
v3.14.0 source: the flows battery (all fifteen flows
plus the anim_follow negatives), the interface
battery, and the plain-layer and search batteries
all pass on both versions. Proof picture:
demo/anim_follow_final.png — the reference build's
pair at frame 24, halfway through the leader's move:
the red leader mid-travel with the orange follower
riding on top, the gap held.
