# Blender Buddy v3.71.0 — animation's sixty-fourth piece: turning to the other side

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release. The
public download still serves v3.20.0.

**Make it turn to the other side** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first sixty-three animation
pieces — Buddy's sixty-fourth animation piece, teaching
turning to the other side, in four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will turn to the other side** — one thing that will
   turn a quarter turn the other way and face sideways the other way.
2. **Remember its starting facing** — a facing
   memory at frame 1, level, rotation Z 0.
3. **Turn it a quarter turn to the other side and keep it turned** —
   rotation Z to -1.5708 remembered at frame 24, and
   the same turned facing remembered again at frame 48,
   side axes staying level and the place held.
4. **Watch it turn to the other side, then save** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the facing memories
   in it.

The teaching point is that the other side is the opposite quarter turn:
the turn-to-the-side piece turned +1.5708 one way — this piece turns
-1.5708 the other way, reading facings
(0, 0, 0) / (0, 0, -1.5708) at frames
1/24/48 in the reference build ((0, 0, -1.5708) at frame 36 as well). The checks sample the real evaluated facing
across the span: the span must run at least 20 frames,
the end turn on the upright axis must land between -1.9 and -1.25, the side axes must hold within 0.35, and the
place must stay within 0.5. A probe on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5
before building showed the facing memories level
at frame 1 and turned -1.5708 on the Z axis at frames 24 and 48 read back (span 47, ends
-1.5708, side 0.0, drift 0.0 in the probe); a turn to the first side read ends 1.5708,
a turn around read ends 3.1416 and -3.1416 (neither wraps into the bar), a spin read ends -6.2832, a wag read ends 0.0, a small turn read ends -0.4, a tilt read ends 0.0 with side drift 1.0, and a turn that returned
read ends 0.0 — all failing the bar. The probe ran on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 with byte-identical outputs.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a starting
facing alone does not prove the turn to the other side, a turn to the first side does not
prove it, a turn around does not prove it, a spin does not prove it, a wag does not prove it, a small turn does not prove it, a tilt does not prove it, and a turn that returns does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 at the final
v3.71.0 source: the flows battery (all seventy-two
flows plus the anim_othersideturn negatives), the interface
battery, and the plain-layer and search batteries all
pass on both versions. Proof picture: demo/anim_othersideturn_final.png — the
reference build's red thing at frame 36, turned to the other side
in its own place on the ground.
