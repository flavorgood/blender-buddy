# Blender Buddy v3.24.0 — animation's seventeenth piece: rolling

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release.

**Make it roll** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first sixteen animation pieces —
Buddy's seventeenth animation piece, teaching rolling, in
four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will roll** — one thing that will
   travel and turn over together.
2. **Remember where it starts, and which way it faces** —
   a place memory and a facing memory together at frame 1,
   resting on the ground two units to one side, facing
   level.
3. **Roll it across** — the far end remembered at frame
   48: four units of travel with four radians of turn,
   paced to each other.
4. **Watch it roll, then save it** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the place and facing
   memories in it.

The teaching point is that travel and turning can be one
motion: a thing resting one unit above the ground rolls
about one unit for every radian it turns — at frame 24
the thing reads (-0.0638, 0, 1) turned -1.9362 radians,
mid-tumble. The checks read the real evaluated place and
facing at the first and last memories: the span must run
at least 20 frames, the travel at least 2.0 units, the
turn at least 2.5 radians, and the travel per radian
between 0.4 and 2.5. A probe on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5
before building showed the place and facing memories
across frames 1/48 read back and interpolate identically
on both versions (travel 4.0, turn 4.0, ratio 1.0 in the
probe; the shipped check reads the same: span 47,
travel 4.0, turn 4.0, ratio 1.0).

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a remembered start alone
does not prove the roll, a slide (travel without turning)
does not prove it, and a spin on the spot (turning
without travel) does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 AND 4.2.5 at the final v3.24.0
source: the flows battery (all twenty-five flows plus
the anim_roll negatives), the interface battery, and
the plain-layer and search batteries all pass on both
versions. Proof picture: demo/anim_roll_final.png —
the reference build's red thing at frame 24, tipped on
its corner mid-tumble across the ground.
