# Blender Buddy v3.79.0 — animation's seventy-second piece: tilting the other way

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release. The
public download still serves v3.20.0.

**Make it tilt the other way** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first seventy-one animation
pieces — Buddy's seventy-second animation piece, teaching
tilting the other way, in four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will tilt the other way** — one thing that will
   lean over the other way.
2. **Remember its starting facing, upright** — a facing
   memory at frame 1, level, rotation (0, 0, 0).
3. **Lean it over the other way and keep it leaned** —
   the leaned facing remembered at frame 48 at -1.0
   on the tilt axis, leaned the other way,
   the other axes staying level and the place held.
4. **Watch it tilt the other way, then save it** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the facing memories
   in it.

The teaching point is that direction is part of the tilt:
the tilt piece's checker took the largest absolute lean on the side axes, so it could not tell the two directions apart — this piece's checker reads the signed end lean on the tilt axis, so the first way reads +1.0 while the other way reads -1.0, reading facings
(0, 0, 0) / (-0.484, 0, 0) / (-1.0, 0, 0) at frames
1/24/48 in the reference build. The checks sample the real evaluated facing
across the span: the span must run at least 20 frames,
the signed end lean must land between -1.8 and -0.6, the peak lean must stay within 2.2, the other axes must hold within 0.35, and the
thing must stay within 0.5 of its place. A probe on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5
before building showed the other-way reference read span 47, signed end lean -1.0, peak 1.0, Y drift 0.0, Z drift 0.0, place drift 0.0; a first-way tilt read signed end lean +1.0,
a small lean the other way read -0.25, an over-lean the other way read -2.6 with peak 2.6, a rock the other way that settles read end lean 0.0, a spin read end lean 0.0 with Z drift 6.2832, a flip the other way read end lean -6.2832 with peak 6.2832, a lean on the Y axis read end lean 0.0 with Y drift 1.0, and a tilt the other way that moved read end lean -1.0 with place drift 3.0 — all failing the bar. The probe ran on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 with byte-identical outputs.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a starting
facing alone does not prove the tilt the other way, a first-way tilt does not
prove it, a small lean the other way does not prove it, an over-lean the other way does not prove it, a rock that settles does not prove it, a spin does not prove it, and a lean on the Y axis does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 at the final
v3.79.0 source: the flows battery (all eighty
flows plus the anim_othertilt negatives), the interface
battery, and the plain-layer and search batteries all
pass on both versions. Proof picture: demo/anim_othertilt_final.png — the
reference build's red thing at frame 48, leaned the other way
at facing (-1.0, 0, 0) in its own place.
