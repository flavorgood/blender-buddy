# Blender Buddy v3.22.0 — animation's fifteenth piece: stretching

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release.

**Make it stretch** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first fourteen animation pieces —
Buddy's fifteenth animation piece, teaching stretching, in
four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will stretch** — one thing whose
   shape will lengthen one way.
2. **Remember its starting size** — a size memory at
   frame 1 that the stretch is measured from.
3. **Stretch it along one way** — one axis at 2.5 times
   its start remembered at frame 48, the other two axes
   held at their starting sizes.
4. **Watch it stretch, then save it** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with both size memories in it.

The teaching point is that a size journey can be uneven:
one direction travels and the others stay, and Blender
fills in the lengthening between them — halfway across,
at frame 24, the long axis reads 1.7261 times its start
while the others still read 1.0. The checks read the size
memories themselves and the real evaluated size at the
first and last memories: the span must run at least 20
frames, the longest axis must reach at least 1.8 times
its start, and the other axes must stay within 0.3 of
theirs. A probe on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 before
building showed size memories at (1,1,1) and (2.5,1,1)
across frames 1/48 read back and interpolate identically
on both versions.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a starting size alone
does not prove the stretch, a grow that swells every
axis together does not prove it either, and a small tug
does not prove it.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 AND 4.2.5 at the final v3.22.0
source: the flows battery (all twenty-three flows plus
the anim_stretch negatives), the interface battery, and
the plain-layer and search batteries all pass on both
versions. Proof picture: demo/anim_stretch_final.png —
the reference build's red thing at frame 48, stretched
to two and a half times its length along one axis, its
other sizes unchanged, on the ground.
