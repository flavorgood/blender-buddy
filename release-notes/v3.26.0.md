# Blender Buddy v3.26.0 — animation's nineteenth piece: rising

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release.

**Make it rise** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first eighteen animation pieces —
Buddy's nineteenth animation piece, teaching rising, in
four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will rise** — one thing that will
   climb.
2. **Remember where it starts, on the ground** — a place
   memory at frame 1 at ground level, its centre one unit
   up for the usual two-unit thing.
3. **Let it rise up high and stay there** — the high end
   remembered at frame 48 in the air, its centre at least
   three units up.
4. **Watch it rise, then save it** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the place memories in it.

The teaching point is that a rise is a one-way trip, the
mirror of the fall: the fall came down and stayed, a rise
goes up and stays — at frame 24 the thing reads
(0, 0, 2.4521), partway up between the ground and its
high end. The checks read the real evaluated place at
the first and last memories: the span must run at least
20 frames, the rise at least 1.5 units, the start height
at most 1.3 so the thing truly starts on the ground, the
end height at least 3.0 so it truly ends up high, and
the sideways drift at most half the rise, so a rise goes
up, not sideways. A probe on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5
before building showed the place memories across frames
1/48 read back and interpolate identically on both
versions (span 47, rise 3.0, start height 1.0, end
height 4.0, drift 0.0 in the probe; the shipped check
reads the same); a fall read rise -3.0, a slide on the
ground read rise 0.0, a small lift read rise 0.5, and a
rise drifting 2.0 sideways over a 3.0 rise read drift
2.0 — all failing the bars.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a ground start alone
does not prove the rise, a fall does not prove it, and
a small lift (0.5 of rise) does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 AND 4.2.5 at the final v3.26.0
source: the flows battery (all twenty-seven flows plus
the anim_rise negatives), the interface battery, and
the plain-layer and search batteries all pass on both
versions. Proof picture: demo/anim_rise_final.png —
the reference build's red thing at frame 24, partway up
in the air, with its shadow below on the ground.
