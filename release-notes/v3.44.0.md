# Blender Buddy v3.44.0 — animation's thirty-seventh piece: wagging

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release.

**Make it wag** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first thirty-six animation
pieces — Buddy's thirty-seventh animation piece, teaching
wagging, in four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will wag** — one thing that will
   turn a little one way and the other, again and again.
2. **Remember its starting facing** — a facing memory at
   frame 1, level on every axis.
3. **Wag it side to side and settle it forward** — turned
   0.4 radians one way remembered at frame 13, turned
   0.4 the other way at frame 25, turned 0.4 the first
   way again at frame 37, and facing forward again at
   frame 48, in its own place the whole way.
4. **Watch it wag, then save it** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the facing memories
   in it.

The teaching point is that a wag is the turn and the
turning back, both ways together: the nod leaned a
thing a little to one side only and repeated, and the
spin turned a thing the whole way round — a wag turns
a little, both ways, and repeats, reading facings
0.0 / 0.4 / -0.4 / 0.4 / 0.0 at frames 1/13/25/37/48 in
the reference build. The checks sample the real
evaluated facing across the span: the span must run at
least 20 frames, the turn must reach between 0.3 and
0.65 one way and between 0.3 and 0.65 the other way,
the turn must change direction at least three times,
the ends must land within 0.3 of the starting facing,
the side axes must hold within 0.2, and the thing must
stay within 0.5 of its place. A probe on Blender 5.3.0
and 4.2.5 before building showed the facing memories
level at frame 1, turned 0.4 on the Z axis at frame
13, turned -0.4 at frame 25, turned 0.4 again at frame
37 and level again at frame 48 read back and
interpolate identically on both versions (span 47,
high 0.4, low -0.4, reversals 3, ends 0.0, side drift
0.0, place drift 0.0 in the probe); a spin read high
6.2832 with ends 6.2832, a nod read high 0.0 with side
drift 0.5, a sway read high 0.0 with side drift 0.8, a
small wag read high 0.15 and low -0.15, a single swing
read low 0.0 with reversals 1, and a one-way turn read
low 0.0 with reversals 0 and ends 0.4 — all failing
the bar.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a starting facing
alone does not prove the wag, a spin does not prove
it, a nod does not prove it, a sway does not prove it,
a small wag does not prove it, and a single swing does
not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 at the final
v3.44.0 source: the flows battery (all forty-five
flows plus the anim_wag negatives), the interface
battery, and the plain-layer and search batteries all
pass. Proof picture: demo/anim_wag_final.png — the
reference build's red thing at frame 13, turned a
little one way at 0.4 radians at the first turn of
its wag.
