# Blender Buddy v3.23.0 — animation's sixteenth piece: orbiting

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release.

**Make it orbit** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first fifteen animation pieces —
Buddy's sixteenth animation piece, teaching orbiting, in
four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will orbit** — one thing whose
   place will circle a centre.
2. **Remember its starting place** — a place memory at
   frame 1, out at the edge of the circle (three units
   from the middle).
3. **Go all the way around** — the other quarter points
   of the same circle remembered at frames 13, 25 and
   37, and home to the starting place at frame 48.
4. **Watch it orbit, then save it** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the place memories in it.

The teaching point is that a journey can be a ring: the
same distance from one centre the whole way round, and
Blender fills in the ring between the quarter points —
at frame 24 the thing reads (-2.9696, 0.3741), just
short of the far side. The checks read the real
evaluated place sampled across the whole span: it must
run at least 20 frames, the angle travelled around the
samples' own middle must reach at least 5.0 radians,
the average radius must be at least 1.5, the radius
variation must stay at most 0.5, and the ends must meet
back at the start. A probe on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5
before building showed the quarter-point memories
across frames 1/13/25/37/48 read back and interpolate
identically on both versions (sweep 6.2594, mean radius
2.8693, variation 0.1784 in the probe; the shipped
check, sampling the evaluated place, reads span 47,
sweep 6.2832, mean radius 2.8673, variation 0.248,
ends gap 0.0).

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a starting place alone
does not prove the orbit, a straight move does not
prove it, and a quarter arc does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 AND 4.2.5 at the final v3.23.0
source: the flows battery (all twenty-four flows plus
the anim_orbit negatives), the interface battery, and
the plain-layer and search batteries all pass on both
versions. Proof picture: demo/anim_orbit_final.png —
the reference build's red thing at frame 25, on the far
side of its circle from its start, with the blue centre
marker on the ground between.
