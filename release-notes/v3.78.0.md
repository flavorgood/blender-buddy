# Blender Buddy v3.78.0 — animation's seventy-first piece: flipping the other way

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release. The
public download still serves v3.20.0.

**Make it flip the other way** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first seventy animation
pieces — Buddy's seventy-first animation piece, teaching
flipping the other way, in four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will flip the other way** — one thing that will
   go all the way over the other way.
2. **Remember its upright facing** — a facing
   memory at frame 1, level, rotation (0, 0, 0).
3. **Flip it all the way over the other way, and land it** —
   the upside-down facing remembered at frame 24 at -3.1416
   on the flip axis, reached the other way round,
   and the upright facing remembered again at frame 48
   at -6.2832, one full turn the other way,
   the other axes staying level and the place held.
4. **Watch it flip the other way, then save it** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the facing memories
   in it.

The teaching point is that direction is part of the flip:
the flip piece's checker totalled the absolute turn, so it could not tell the two directions apart — this piece's checker reads the signed end turn on the flip axis, so the first way reads +6.2832 while the other way reads -6.2832, reading facings
(0, 0, 0) / (-3.1416, 0, 0) / (-5.3145, 0, 0) / (-6.2832, 0, 0) at frames
1/24/36/48 in the reference build. The checks sample the real evaluated facing
across the span: the span must run at least 20 frames,
the signed end turn must land between -6.6 and -5.9, the other axes must hold within 0.35, and the
thing must stay within 0.5 of its place. A probe on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5
before building showed the other-way reference read span 47, signed end turn -6.2832, side drift 0.0, place drift 0.0; a first-way flip read signed end turn +6.2832,
a half flip the other way read -3.1416, a small turn the other way read -0.4, a flip on another axis read end turn 0.0 with side drift 6.2832, a tilt read end turn 0.0 with side drift 1.0, a flip the other way that returned read end turn 0.0, and a flip the other way that moved read end turn -6.2832 with place drift 3.0 — all failing the bar. The probe ran on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 with byte-identical outputs.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a starting
facing alone does not prove the flip the other way, a first-way flip does not
prove it, a half flip the other way does not prove it, a small turn the other way does not prove it, a flip on another axis does not prove it, a tilt does not prove it, and a flip the other way that returns does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 at the final
v3.78.0 source: the flows battery (all seventy-nine
flows plus the anim_otherflip negatives), the interface
battery, and the plain-layer and search batteries all
pass on both versions. Proof picture: demo/anim_otherflip_final.png — the
reference build's red cone at frame 24, upside down the other way
at facing (-3.1416, 0, 0) in its own place.
