# Blender Buddy v3.29.0 — animation's twenty-second piece: shaking

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release.

**Make it shake** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first twenty-one animation pieces —
Buddy's twenty-second animation piece, teaching shaking, in
four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will shake** — one thing that will
   rattle in place.
2. **Remember where it starts, resting home** — a place
   memory at frame 1, the thing resting where it lives.
3. **Shake it side to side and settle it home** — swung out
   to each side in turn across the span, remembered in turn,
   and home again at the last frame.
4. **Watch it shake, then save it** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the place memories in it.

The teaching point is that a shake is a rattle, not a
journey: the thing never leaves home — it swings out and
back, again and again, at its home height, and ends resting
where it started — crossing home at frame 15 on its way
from one side to the other. The checks sample the real
evaluated place across the span: the span must run at
least 20 frames, the travel on the busiest sideways axis
at least 1.0 unit, the direction of travel must change at
least 3 times, the ends within 0.3 of the start, and the
height held within 0.35 the whole way. A probe on
Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 before building showed the place
memories across frames 1/10/19/29/38/48 read back and
interpolate identically on both versions (span 47,
travel 1.4, reversals 4, ends gap 0.0, height variation
0.0 in the probe); a one-way move read reversals 0 with
ends gap 3.0, a bounce read sideways travel 0.0 with
height variation 2.0, and a small wiggle read travel
0.4 — all failing the bar.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a home place alone
does not prove the shake, a one-way move does not prove
it, a bounce does not prove it, and a small wiggle does
not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 AND 4.2.5 at the final v3.29.0
source: the flows battery (all thirty flows plus
the anim_shake negatives), the interface battery, and
the plain-layer and search batteries all pass on both
versions. Proof picture: demo/anim_shake_final.png —
the reference build's red thing at frame 19, swung
fully out to one side of its home place, the blue home
marker on the ground where it started.
