# Blender Buddy v3.46.0 — animation's thirty-ninth piece: bobbing

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release.

**Make it bob** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first thirty-eight animation
pieces — Buddy's thirty-ninth animation piece, teaching
bobbing, in four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will bob** — one thing that will
   go up and down and settle back on the ground.
2. **Remember where it starts, on the ground** — a place
   memory at frame 1.
3. **Bob it up and down and settle it back on the ground** — raised
   one unit remembered at frame 13, back down at
   frame 25, raised one unit again at frame 37, back
   down at frame 48, in its own place the whole way.
4. **Watch it bob, then save it** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the place memories
   in it.

The teaching point is that a bob is the up-and-down
twice that settles: the bounce piece sent a thing up
once and brought it back down, and the hop piece sent
it up and across — a bob rises a little, settles,
rises again, and settles back on the ground in its own
place, reading places 0 / 1 / 0 / 1 / 0 in height above
its ground start at frames 1/13/25/37/48 in
the reference build (probe places 1 / 2 / 1 / 2 / 1
for a centre starting one unit up). The checks sample
the real evaluated place across the span: the span
must run at least 20 frames, the peak rise must land
between 0.7 and 1.4, the height must change direction
at least three times, the ends must sit within 0.3 of
each other in height, and the thing must stay within
0.5 sideways of its start place. A probe on Blender
5.3.0 and 4.2.5 before building showed the place
memories at (0, 0, 1), (0, 0, 2), (0, 0, 1), (0, 0, 2)
and (0, 0, 1) across frames 1/13/25/37/48 read back
and interpolate identically on both versions (span 47,
peak 1.0, reversals 3, ends 0.0, sideways 0.0 in the
probe); a single bounce read peak 2.5 with reversals
1, a rise read peak 3.0 with reversals 0 and ends 3.0,
a hover read ends 3.0, a small bob read peak 0.3, a
slide read peak 0.0 with sideways 3.0, and a hop read
peak 2.0 with sideways 3.0 — all failing the bar.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a starting place
alone does not prove the bob, a single bounce does not
prove it, a rise does not prove it, a small bob does
not prove it, and a slide does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 at the final
v3.46.0 source: the flows battery (all forty-seven
flows plus the anim_bob negatives), the interface
battery, and the plain-layer and search batteries all
pass. Proof picture: demo/anim_bob_final.png — the
reference build's red thing at frame 13, raised one
unit at the top of its first bob, in the air above
its ground place with its shadow below.
