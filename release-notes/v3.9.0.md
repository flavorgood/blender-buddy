# Blender Buddy v3.9.0 — animation's second piece: easing and curves

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes.

**Make it glide** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first animation pieces — Buddy's
second animation piece, teaching the pace of a journey
and how a middle memory steers it, in four steps:

1. **Start with a move that travels** — the first
   piece's raw material: two remembered places and a
   real journey between them.
2. **Slow the ends, keep the middle** — easing: the
   smooth (Bezier) curve with an easing chosen on the
   memories, so the journey leaves gently, travels at
   full pace, and settles in instead of slamming at
   both ends.
3. **Bend the journey with a middle memory** — a third
   memory, placed halfway and off the straight line:
   the journey must be there at that frame, so the
   path and the timing both bend around it.
4. **Watch it glide, then save it** — played back from
   the start, and the file saved with the curve and
   all three memories still on the thing.

The eased step is checked two ways, because a probe on
Blender 4.2.5 before building showed Blender's own
default curve is already gentle at the ends (leaving at
0.18 of middle pace, arriving at 0.22) — a pace-only
check would tick for free, for a learner who had chosen
nothing. So the tick first reads the memories
themselves: every location key must carry the smooth
curve with a real easing picked for it, never the
automatic one. Then it reads the real pace the way a
viewer feels it — the play line at the span's edges,
fifths and middle — and demands the ends at no more
than three quarters of the middle's pace. On the
reference build, after the bend, the journey leaves at
0.56 of middle pace and arrives at 0.67.

The bend step reads the middle memory's evaluated
place against the straight line between the journey's
first and last places, at the middle memory's own
moment, and demands a clear departure — at least a
quarter of a pace off the line.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks no steps at all, a travelling move
left on Blender's default curve does not prove the
eased step, and an eased but straight two-memory
journey does not prove the bend.

The Start screen's coverage box now names both
animation pieces — "Make it move" and "Make it glide"
— and still says plainly that they are first pieces of
the craft, not the whole of it; cameras and characters
remain later pieces, and the other crafts remain
untaught.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5: the flows battery
(all ten flows walked end to end, anim_ease included,
plus its three negative cases and the anim_move and
sculpting negatives), the interface battery (including
the coverage box), and the plain-layer and search
batteries. The full parallel battery (test_tour)
remains owed on an unloaded machine, as recorded in
the lane queue.
