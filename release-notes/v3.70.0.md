# Blender Buddy v3.70.0 — animation's sixty-third piece: moving back diagonally the other way

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release. The
public download still serves v3.20.0.

**Make it move back diagonally the other way** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first sixty-two animation
pieces — Buddy's sixty-third animation piece, teaching
moving back diagonally the other way, in four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will move back diagonally the other way** — one thing that will
   travel backward and sideways the other way together.
2. **Remember where it starts, on the ground** — a place
   memory at frame 1, location (0, 0, 1).
3. **Move it back diagonally the other way (backward and sideways the other way) and keep it there** —
   place (-1.5, -1.5, 1) remembered at frame 24, and
   place (-3, -3, 1) remembered at frame 48, on the
   ground the whole way.
4. **Watch it move back diagonally the other way, then save** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the place memories
   in it.

The teaching point is that a back-other diagonal is both travels
together: a slide the other way travels sideways the other way only and a backward
move travels backward only — a back-other diagonal goes both ways
at once, staying on the ground, reading places
(0, 0, 1) / (-1.5, -1.5, 1) / (-3, -3, 1) at frames
1/24/48 in the reference build ((-2.5375, -2.5375, 1) partway across at frame 36). The checks sample the real evaluated place
across the span: the span must run at least 20 frames,
the backward travel must land between 2.2 and 3.8, the sideways-other travel must land between 2.2 and 3.8, no sampled place may sit more than 0.35 above or below the start height, and the
end height must sit within 0.35 of the start height. A probe on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5
before building showed the place memories on the
ground at frame 1, halfway backward and halfway across the other way
at frame 24 and far behind and far across the other way at frame 48 read back (span 47, backward
3.0, sideways_other 3.0, peak 0.0, trough 0.0, end deviation 0.0 in the probe); a slide the other way read backward 0.0,
a backward-only move read sideways_other 0.0, a leap read peak 2.0, a small back-other-diagonal read backward 1.0 with sideways_other 1.0, a back-other-diagonal that returned read backward 0.0 with sideways_other 0.0, and a back-other-diagonal up in the air
read peak 2.0 with end deviation 2.0 — all failing the bar. The probe ran on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 with byte-identical outputs.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a starting
place alone does not prove the back-other diagonal, a slide the other way does not
prove it, a backward-only move does not prove it, a leap does not prove it, a small back-other-diagonal does not prove it, and a back-other-diagonal that returns does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 at the final
v3.70.0 source: the flows battery (all seventy-one
flows plus the anim_backotherdiagonal negatives), the interface
battery, and the plain-layer and search batteries all
pass on both versions. Proof picture: demo/anim_backotherdiagonal_final.png — the
reference build's red thing at frame 24, mid-back-other-diagonal
at (-1.5, -1.5, 1), on the ground.
