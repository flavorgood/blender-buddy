# Blender Buddy v3.7.0 — sculpting's fifth piece: Multires

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes.

**Work it at every level** sits under "Learn by doing" beside
the first four sculpting pieces — Buddy's fifth sculpting
piece, teaching Multires (one sculpt held at several levels
of detail at once) the way sculptors actually use it, in
five steps:

1. **Start with tidy clay** — a finer, even ball: Multires
   divides the net you start with, so the net matters and
   never goes away.
2. **Give it levels** — a Multires modifier, subdivided
   three times; the tick reads the modifier itself (at
   least two real levels built).
3. **Block the big forms low** — sculpt level stepped down
   to 1, big brushes, big forms only; the tick reads the
   clay worked, with its levels still on it.
4. **Work the fine detail high** — sculpt level at the top,
   small and light; the tick reads the surface's fine grain
   past a probed bar big smooth forms alone cannot reach
   (0.0037 vs 0.0069 on the reference clay; bar 0.0055).
5. **Finish, and keep the levels** — smooth shading and the
   file saved, the Multires modifier left on the clay,
   unapplied: applying it would throw the levels away.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk: a
plain ball ticks only step 1, levels with no work tick
neither worked step, big forms alone do not prove fine
detail, and detail worked without Multires does not prove
the detail step either.

The Start screen's coverage box now names all five
sculpting pieces and still says plainly that they are first
pieces of the craft, not the whole of it; animation and the
other crafts remain untaught. With Multires taught, every
way a sculpt's surface is organised — clay, mirror, DynTopo,
masks/face sets, levels — now has a guided piece.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0: the flows battery (all eight
flows walked end to end, sculpt_multires included, plus its
four negative cases and the masks, DynTopo and symmetry
negatives), the interface battery (including the coverage
box), and the plain-layer and search batteries. The 4.2.5
binary was not present on the build machine this run, so
the 4.2.5 pass is still owed; the full parallel battery
(test_tour) remains owed on an unloaded machine, as
recorded in the lane queue.
