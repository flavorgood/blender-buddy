# Blender Buddy v3.28.0 — animation's twenty-first piece: spinning

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release.

**Make it spin** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first twenty animation pieces —
Buddy's twenty-first animation piece, teaching spinning, in
four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will spin** — one thing that will
   turn in place.
2. **Remember its starting facing** — a facing memory at
   frame 1, level on every axis.
3. **Spin it all the way around and remember** — half a
   turn remembered at frame 24, one full turn at frame 48.
4. **Watch it spin, then save it** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the facing memories in it.

The teaching point is that a spin is a turntable turn: the
flip went over the side, a spin goes around the upright
axis, stays upright, stays in place, and comes home facing
the same way — at frame 12 the thing reads 0.9175 rad, a
quarter of the way around. The checks sample the real
evaluated facing across the span: the span must run at
least 20 frames, the total turn on the upright axis at
least 5.0 rad, the tilt on the other axes within 0.35 rad,
the ends landing on the same facing allowing whole turns,
and the thing within half a unit of its start. A probe on
Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 before building showed the facing
memories across frames 1/24/48 read back and interpolate
identically on both versions (span 47, turn 6.2832, ends
gap 0.0 in the probe); a half spin read turn 3.1416 with
ends gap 3.1416, a rock back and forth read turn 2.0, and
a flip on the side axis read upright-axis turn 0.0 — all
failing the bar.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a starting facing alone
does not prove the spin, a half spin does not prove it,
and a flip does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 AND 4.2.5 at the final v3.28.0
source: the flows battery (all twenty-nine flows plus
the anim_spin negatives), the interface battery, and
the plain-layer and search batteries all pass on both
versions. Proof picture: demo/anim_spin_final.png —
the reference build's red thing at frame 12, turned
0.9175 rad on its way around, upright on the ground.
