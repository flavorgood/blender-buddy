# Blender Buddy v3.40.0 — animation's thirty-third piece: gliding

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release.

**Make it glide far** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first thirty-two animation pieces —
Buddy's thirty-third animation piece, teaching gliding, in
four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will glide** — one thing that will
   travel far through the air.
2. **Remember where it starts, up high** — a place
   memory at frame 1, its centre at least 3 units up.
3. **Glide it far across, sinking only a little** — halfway
   across and only half a unit lower remembered at frame
   24, and the far end — three units across and only one
   unit down — remembered at frame 48, still in the air.
4. **Watch it glide, then save it** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the place memories in it.

The teaching point is that a glide is the far and the
nearly-level together: the descend brought a thing all
the way down to the ground as it travelled — a glide
stays flying, reading (0.4381, 0, 3.854) partway across
at frame 12 and (1.5, 0, 3.5) halfway across at frame 24
in the reference build's own places. The checks sample
the real evaluated place across the span: the span must
run at least 20 frames, the travel between the ends must
reach at least 2.5, the drop must sit between 0.7 and
1.8, the end height must stay at least 2.2, and the
height must never rise back by more than 0.35 in a step.
A probe on Blender 4.2.5 before building showed the place
memories at (0, 0, 4), (1.5, 0, 3.5) and (3, 0, 3) across
frames 1/24/48 read back as span 47, travel 3.0, drop
1.0, end 3.0, rises 0 in the probe; a fall on the spot
read travel 0.0 with drop 3.0, a diagonal descend read
drop 3.0 with end 1.0, a slide at height read drop 0.0, a
hop read end 1.0 with drop 3.0, and a small glide read
travel 1.0 with drop 0.3 — all failing the bar. No
Blender 5.3.0 binary is on this cell this run, so the
5.3.0 battery pass for this piece is owed (the land piece
had the same gap on its run).

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a high starting place
alone does not prove the glide, a fall on the spot does
not prove it, a diagonal descend does not prove it, a
slide at height does not prove it, a hop does not prove
it, and a small glide does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 4.2.5 at the final v3.40.0 source:
the flows battery (all forty-one flows plus the
anim_glide negatives), the interface battery, and the
plain-layer and search batteries all pass. Proof picture:
demo/anim_glide_final.png — the reference build's red
thing at frame 24, in the air halfway across its glide
with its shadow below.
