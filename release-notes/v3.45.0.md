# Blender Buddy v3.45.0 — animation's thirty-eighth piece: turning around

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release.

**Make it turn around** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first thirty-seven animation
pieces — Buddy's thirty-eighth animation piece, teaching
turning around, in four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will turn around** — one thing that will
   turn the whole half circle and face the other way.
2. **Remember its starting facing** — a facing memory at
   frame 1, level on every axis.
3. **Turn it right around and leave it facing the other way** — turned
   3.1416 radians on the upright axis remembered at
   frame 48, in its own place the whole way.
4. **Watch it turn, then save it** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the facing memories
   in it.

The teaching point is that a turn around is the half
circle that stays turned: the wag turned a thing a
little, both ways, and settled it facing forward, and
the spin turned a thing the whole way round back to
facing forward — a turn around stops halfway and
leaves the thing facing the other way, reading facings
0.0 / 1.5207 / 3.1416 at frames 1/24/48 in
the reference build. The checks sample the real
evaluated facing across the span: the span must run at
least 20 frames, the end turn on the upright axis must
land between 2.8 and 3.45, the side axes must hold
within 0.35, and the thing must stay within 0.5 of
its place. A probe on Blender 5.3.0
and 4.2.5 before building showed the facing memories
level at frame 1 and turned 3.1416 on the Z axis at frame
48 read back and
interpolate identically on both versions (span 47,
ends 3.1416, side drift
0.0, place drift 0.0 in the probe); a spin read ends
6.2832, a wag read ends 0.0, a small turn read ends
0.4, a tilt read ends 0.0 with side drift 1.0, a sway
read side drift 0.8, and a nod read side drift 0.5 — all failing
the bar.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a starting facing
alone does not prove the turn, a spin does not prove
it, a wag does not prove it, a small turn does not prove it,
and a tilt does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 at the final
v3.45.0 source: the flows battery (all forty-six
flows plus the anim_turn negatives), the interface
battery, and the plain-layer and search batteries all
pass. Proof picture: demo/anim_turn_final.png — the
reference build's red thing at frame 48, turned right
around at 3.1416 radians and facing the other way.
