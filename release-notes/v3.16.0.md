# Blender Buddy v3.16.0 — animation's ninth piece: bouncing

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes.

**Make it bounce** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first eight animation pieces —
Buddy's ninth animation piece, teaching bouncing, in
four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will bounce** — one thing that
   will leave the ground.
2. **Remember where it starts, down low** — a place
   memory at frame 1, holding the low place so the high
   place has something to rise from and return to.
3. **Send it up, and bring it back down** — a high place
   memory at frame 24 (the reference build raises it two
   and a half units) and the low place remembered again
   at frame 48.
4. **Watch it bounce, then save it** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with all three place
   memories in it.

The teaching point is that a bounce is three memories,
not two: low, high, and low again — a ball bouncing, a
character hopping, a crate dropped and rebounding — and
Blender fills in the rise and the fall between. The
checks read the place memories themselves and the real
evaluated height across the whole span: the span must
run at least 20 frames, the peak must really clear the
ground — at least one and a half units above the lower
end (the reference build's peak is 2.5, reading about
1.17 a quarter of the way up at frame 12) — and the two
ends must sit at about the same height. A probe on
Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 before building showed a
low-high-low set of memories reads back and interpolates
identically on both versions.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a starting place alone
does not prove the bounce, a straight rise that never
comes back down does not prove it, and a small hop
(half a unit) does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 AND 4.2.5 at the final
v3.16.0 source: the flows battery (all seventeen flows
plus the anim_bounce negatives), the interface battery,
and the plain-layer and search batteries all pass on
both versions. Proof picture: demo/anim_bounce_final.png
— the reference build's red thing at frame 24, at the
top of its bounce, in the air above the ground.
