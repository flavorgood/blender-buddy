# Blender Buddy v3.36.0 — animation's twenty-ninth piece: climbing

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release.

**Make it climb** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first twenty-eight animation pieces —
Buddy's twenty-ninth animation piece, teaching climbing, in
four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will climb** — one thing that will
   go up and forward together.
2. **Remember where it starts, on the ground** — a place
   memory at frame 1, its centre one unit up.
3. **Climb it diagonally, up and forward in steps** — one
   unit forward and one unit up remembered at frame 17,
   another step remembered at frame 33, and the top —
   three units forward and three units up — remembered
   at frame 48.
4. **Watch it climb, then save it** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the place memories in it.

The teaching point is that a climb is the up and the
forward together: the rise sent a thing straight up, and
the slide sent it straight across the ground — a climb
does both at once, step by step, and never drops back
down, reading (1.4344, 0, 2.4344) partway up at frame 24
in the reference build's own places. The checks sample
the real evaluated place across the span: the span must
run at least 20 frames, the rise from the start height
to the end height must reach at least 2.5, the forward
travel between the ends must reach at least 2.5, and the
height must never drop back by more than 0.35 in a step.
A probe on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 before building showed
the place memories at (0, 0, 1), (1, 0, 2), (2, 0, 3) and
(3, 0, 4) across frames 1/17/33/48 read back and
interpolate identically on both versions (span 47, rise
3.0, travel 3.0, height drops 0 in the probe); a rise on
the spot read travel 0.0, a slide read rise 0.0, a hop
read rise 0.0, a fall read rise −3.0, and a small climb
read rise 1.0 with travel 1.0 — all failing the bar.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a starting place alone
does not prove the climb, a rise on its own place does
not prove it, a slide does not prove it, a hop does not
prove it, and a small climb does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 AND 4.2.5 at the final v3.36.0
source: the flows battery (all thirty-seven flows plus
the anim_climb negatives), the interface battery, and
the plain-layer and search batteries all pass on both
versions. Proof picture: demo/anim_climb_final.png —
the reference build's red thing at frame 33, partway up
in the air on its climb.
