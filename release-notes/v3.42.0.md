# Blender Buddy v3.42.0 — animation's thirty-fifth piece: bowing

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release.

**Make it bow** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first thirty-four animation
pieces — Buddy's thirty-fifth animation piece, teaching
bowing, in four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will bow** — one thing that will
   lean deep over to one side and come back up.
2. **Remember its starting facing, upright** — a facing
   memory at frame 1, level on every axis.
3. **Bow it over and bring it back upright** — leaned
   0.9 radians one way remembered at frame 24, and
   upright again remembered at frame 48, in its own
   place the whole way.
4. **Watch it bow, then save it** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the facing memories
   in it.

The teaching point is that a bow is the one deep lean
and the coming back together: the tilt leaned a thing
one way and left it there, and the sway leaned it to
both sides — a bow leans one way only and returns,
reading facings 0.0 / 0.9 / 0.0 at frames 1/24/48 in
the reference build. The checks sample the real
evaluated facing across the span: the span must run at
least 20 frames, on the side axis that leans the most
the lean must reach at least 0.6 one way and no more
than 0.15 the other way, the lean must change
direction at least once, the ends must land within
0.3 of level, the upright (Z) facing must hold within
0.35, and the thing must stay within 0.5 of its place.
A probe on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 before building
showed the facing memories level at frame 1, leaned
0.9 on the X axis at frame 24 and level again at
frame 48 read back and interpolate identically on
both versions (span 47, peak 0.9, other way 0.0,
reversals 1, ends 0.0, upright drift 0.0, place drift
0.0 in the probe); a tilt read reversals 0 with ends
0.9, a sway read the other way 0.8, a spin read
upright drift 6.2832, and a small bow read peak 0.2 —
all failing the bar.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, an upright facing
alone does not prove the bow, a tilt does not prove
it, a sway does not prove it, a spin does not prove
it, and a small bow does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 at the final
v3.42.0 source: the flows battery (all forty-three
flows plus the anim_bow negatives), the interface
battery, and the plain-layer and search batteries all
pass. Proof picture: demo/anim_bow_final.png — the
reference build's red thing at frame 24, leaned deep
over to one side at 0.9 radians at the bottom of its
bow.
