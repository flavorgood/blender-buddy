# Blender Buddy v3.17.0 — animation's tenth piece: swinging

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes.

**Make it swing** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first nine animation pieces —
Buddy's tenth animation piece, teaching swinging, in
four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will swing** — one thing that
   will lean both ways.
2. **Remember its facing, leaned one way** — a facing
   (rotation) memory at frame 1, the reference build
   leaning the thing 0.7 radians to one side, holding
   the lean so the other lean has something to swing
   from and return to.
3. **Lean it the other way, and back** — the other lean
   remembered at frame 24 (0.7 radians the other way)
   and the first lean remembered again at frame 48.
4. **Watch it swing, then save it** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with all three facing
   memories in it.

The teaching point is that the memories can hold a
thing's FACING as a journey of its own: leaned, leaned
the other way, and back — a hanging sign, a pendulum, a
character swaying — and Blender fills in the sweep
between. The checks read the facing memories themselves
and the real evaluated facing across the whole span:
the span must run at least 20 frames, the lean must
sweep at least one radian end to end, it must reach
clearly to BOTH sides of upright on the same axis (at
least 0.35 radians each way; the reference build reaches
0.7 each way, reading about −0.05 a quarter of the way
across at frame 12), and the two ends must sit at about
the same lean. A probe on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 before
building showed a lean-lean-back set of memories reads
back and interpolates identically on both versions.

One defect was caught by the proof picture during the
build: the first design leaned on the Z axis, which is
Blender's up axis, so the reference thing spun like a
turntable and the render read upright. The shipped
piece leans on the Y axis, and the lean reads plainly
in the proof picture.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a starting lean alone
does not prove the swing, a one-way turn does not prove
it, a tip that never crosses the middle (0 → 1.2 → 0)
does not prove it, and a small sway does not prove it
either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 AND 4.2.5 at the final
v3.17.0 source: the flows battery (all eighteen flows
plus the anim_swing negatives), the interface battery,
and the plain-layer and search batteries all pass on
both versions. Proof picture: demo/anim_swing_final.png
— the reference build's red thing at frame 24, leaned
fully the other way, tipped on the ground with its
shadow below.
