# Blender Buddy v3.47.0 — animation's fortieth piece: floating

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release.

**Make it float** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first thirty-nine animation
pieces — Buddy's fortieth animation piece, teaching
floating, in four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will float** — one thing that will
   rise a little and stay up, floating.
2. **Remember where it starts, on the ground** — a place
   memory at frame 1, its centre no more than 1.3 units up.
3. **Float it up a little and keep it there** — raised
   one and a half units remembered at frame 24, and the
   same floating place remembered again at frame 48, in
   its own place the whole way.
4. **Watch it float, then save it** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the place memories
   in it.

The teaching point is that a float is the small rise
and the staying together: the hover piece sent a thing
up high and kept it there, and the bob piece went up
and down twice and settled back on the ground — a float
rises just a little, arrives by the middle, and holds,
reading places 0 / 1.5 / 1.5 in height at frames
1/24/48 in the reference build (0.7011 partway up at
frame 12). The checks sample the real evaluated place
across the span: the span must run at least 20 frames,
the rise from the start height to the end height must
land between 1.0 and 2.0, the end height must land
between 1.2 and 2.3, the lowest height from the middle
of the span onward must stay within 0.35 of the end
height, and the thing must stay within 0.5 sideways of
its start place. A probe on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5
before building showed the place memories at
(0, 0, 0), (0, 0, 1.5) and (0, 0, 1.5) across frames
1/24/48 read back and interpolate identically on both
versions (span 47, rise 1.5, end 1.5, hold 1.5, drift
0.0 in the probe); a hover read rise 3.0 with end 3.0,
a bob read rise 0.0 with end 0.0 and hold 0.0, a bounce
read rise 0.0 with end 0.0, a small lift read rise 0.4
with end 0.4, a slide read rise 0.0 with drift 3.0, and
a plain rise read hold 0.7261 — all failing the bar.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a ground starting
place alone does not prove the float, a hover does not
prove it, a bob does not prove it, a bounce does not
prove it, a small lift does not prove it, and a slide
does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 at the final
v3.47.0 source: the flows battery (all forty-eight
flows plus the anim_float negatives), the interface
battery, and the plain-layer and search batteries all
pass. Proof picture: demo/anim_float_final.png — the
reference build's red thing at frame 36, floating a
little above the ground in its own place with its
shadow below.
