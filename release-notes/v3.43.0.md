# Blender Buddy v3.43.0 — animation's thirty-sixth piece: nodding

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release.

**Make it nod** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first thirty-five animation
pieces — Buddy's thirty-sixth animation piece, teaching
nodding, in four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will nod** — one thing that will
   lean a little over to one side and come back, twice.
2. **Remember its starting facing, upright** — a facing
   memory at frame 1, level on every axis.
3. **Nod it twice and settle it upright** — leaned
   0.5 radians one way remembered at frame 13, upright
   at frame 24, leaned 0.5 again at frame 37, and
   upright again at frame 48, in its own place the
   whole way.
4. **Watch it nod, then save it** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the facing memories
   in it.

The teaching point is that a nod is the little lean
and the coming back, twice together: the bow leaned a
thing deep one way once and returned, and the sway
leaned it to both sides — a nod leans one way only, a
little way, and repeats, reading facings 0.0 / 0.5 /
0.0 / 0.5 / 0.0 at frames 1/13/24/37/48 in the
reference build. The checks sample the real evaluated
facing across the span: the span must run at least 20
frames, on the side axis that leans the most the lean
must reach between 0.35 and 0.7 one way and no more
than 0.15 the other way, the lean must change
direction at least three times, the ends must land
within 0.3 of level, the upright (Z) facing must hold
within 0.35, and the thing must stay within 0.5 of
its place. A probe on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 before
building showed the facing memories level at frame 1,
leaned 0.5 on the X axis at frame 13, level at frame
24, leaned 0.5 again at frame 37 and level again at
frame 48 read back and interpolate identically on
both versions (span 47, peak 0.5, other way 0.0,
reversals 3, ends 0.0, upright drift 0.0, place drift
0.0 in the probe); a bow read peak 0.9 with reversals
1, a tilt read reversals 0 with ends 0.5, a sway read
the other way 0.8, a spin read upright drift 6.2832,
a small nod read peak 0.15, and a single nod read
reversals 1 — all failing the bar.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, an upright facing
alone does not prove the nod, a bow does not prove
it, a tilt does not prove it, a sway does not prove
it, a spin does not prove it, a small nod does not
prove it, and a single nod does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 at the final
v3.43.0 source: the flows battery (all forty-four
flows plus the anim_nod negatives), the interface
battery, and the plain-layer and search batteries all
pass. Proof picture: demo/anim_nod_final.png — the
reference build's red thing at frame 13, leaned a
little over to one side at 0.5 radians at the bottom
of its first nod.
