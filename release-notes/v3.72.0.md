# Blender Buddy v3.72.0 — animation's sixty-fifth piece: sidestepping the other way

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release. The
public download still serves v3.20.0.

**Make it sidestep the other way** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first sixty-four animation
pieces — Buddy's sixty-fifth animation piece, teaching
sidestepping the other way, in four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will sidestep the other way** — one thing that will
   step sideways the other way and stay there.
2. **Remember where it starts, on the ground** — a place
   memory at frame 1, centre (0, 0, 1).
3. **Sidestep it the other way and keep it there** —
   place (0, -1.5, 1) remembered at frame 24, and
   place (0, -3, 1) remembered again at frame 48,
   staying on the ground the whole way.
4. **Watch it sidestep the other way, then save** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the place memories
   in it.

The teaching point is that the other way is the opposite sideways step:
the sidestep piece stepped a thing sideways one way — this piece steps
it sideways the other way, reading places
(0, 0, 1) / (0, -1.5, 1) / (0, -3, 1) at frames
1/24/48 in the reference build ((0, -2.5375, 1) at frame 36 as well). The checks sample the real evaluated place
across the span: the span must run at least 20 frames,
the sideways-other travel (the start place's Y minus the end place's Y) must land between 2.2 and 3.8, the forward/backward drift along X must stay within 0.5 across all sampled places, no sampled place may sit more than 0.35 above or below the start height, and the
end height must sit within 0.35 of the start height. A probe on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5
before building showed the place memories
at (0, 0, 1), (0, -1.5, 1) and (0, -3, 1) across frames 1/24/48 read back (span 47, sideways-other
3.0, drift 0.0, peak 0.0, trough 0.0 in the probe); a sidestep the first way read sideways-other -3.0,
a forward slide read sideways-other 0.0 with drift 3.0, a pace that returned read sideways-other 0.0, a small sidestep the other way read sideways-other 1.0, a diagonal read sideways-other 3.0 with drift 3.0, a hop read peak 2.0, and a sidestep the other way that returned
read sideways-other 0.0 — all failing the bar. The probe ran on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 with byte-identical outputs.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a starting
place alone does not prove the sidestep the other way, a sidestep the first way does not
prove it, a forward slide does not prove it, a small sidestep the other way does not prove it, a diagonal does not prove it, a hop does not prove it, and a sidestep the other way that returns does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 at the final
v3.72.0 source: the flows battery (all seventy-three
flows plus the anim_othersidestep negatives), the interface
battery, and the plain-layer and search batteries all
pass on both versions. Proof picture: demo/anim_othersidestep_final.png — the
reference build's red thing at frame 24, halfway across the other way
at (0, -1.5, 1) on the ground.
