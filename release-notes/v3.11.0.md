# Blender Buddy v3.11.0 — animation's fourth piece: characters

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes.

**Make a character move** sits under "Learn by doing" beside
the modeling, sculpting and first three animation pieces
— Buddy's fourth animation piece, teaching characters, in
five steps:

1. **Build a simple character** — a skeleton (an armature)
   of three bones — a body, an arm, a head — with a plain
   part tied to each bone.
2. **Remember its first pose** — the bones' facings
   remembered at frame 1, the way a place is remembered.
3. **Change the pose, and remember again** — one bone
   turned at frame 48, and Blender fills in the swing
   between the two poses.
4. **Make a second part move too** — a different bone,
   posed and remembered the same way, so the character
   performs in parts together.
5. **Watch it move, then save it** — played back, and the
   file saved with the poses still on the skeleton.

The teaching point is the first piece's idea, split into
parts: a character does not move as one thing — it moves
bone by bone, each bone carrying its own two memories.
The checks read the skeleton itself: a real armature with
at least three bones; a bone's facing remembered at the
start; and, for the moved steps, the real turn of each
bone, read from the pose at its first and last remembered
moments (at least 0.3 radians across at least 20 frames).
A probe on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 before building showed
why nothing ticks for free: the factory scene has no
armature at all, and pose bones arrive set to quaternion
turns, so the reference build sets each bone to plain
turns before remembering it.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks no steps, one pose alone does not
prove a moved part, and one part moving alone does not
prove the second part.

The Start screen's coverage box now names all four
animation pieces — "Make it move", "Make it glide",
"Move the camera" and "Make a character move" — and
still says plainly that they are first pieces of the
craft, not the whole of it; the other crafts remain
untaught. With characters taught, animation's four
named pieces are complete.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5: the flows
battery (all twelve flows walked end to end,
anim_character included, plus its three negative cases
and the anim_move, anim_ease, anim_camera and
sculpting negatives), the interface battery (including
the coverage box), and the plain-layer and search
batteries. The full parallel battery (test_tour)
remains owed on an unloaded machine, as recorded in
the lane queue.
