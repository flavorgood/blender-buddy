# Blender Buddy v3.5.0 — sculpting's third piece: DynTopo

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes.

**Detail where you brush** sits under "Learn by doing" beside
"Sculpt it like clay" and "Sculpt both sides at once" — Buddy's
third sculpting piece, teaching DynTopo (Dynamic Topology) the
way sculptors actually use it, in four steps:

1. **Start with coarse clay** — a ball too coarse for the
   detail you want, on purpose: with DynTopo the detail
   arrives where you brush.
2. **Turn DynTopo on** — Constant detail, with its price
   stated plainly in the flow: the tidy net and its UVs are
   gone, so this is exploring and detailing clay, not a
   finished net for painting or animating.
3. **Work one small area** — only that area gains a fine,
   close-packed net; the rest of the clay stays coarse. The
   tick reads the surface's own spacing contrast, so an even
   remesh, however fine, does not tick it.
4. **Turn it off, finish, and save** — smooth shading and
   the file saved, the packed patch and the coarse rest
   exactly as left.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk: a plain
coarse ball ticks only step 1, DynTopo on with no work does
not prove the worked step, and an even fine subdivided
surface does not prove DynTopo work.

The Start screen's coverage box now names all three sculpting
pieces and still says plainly that they are first pieces of
the craft, not the whole of it; animation and the other
crafts remain untaught. Masks/face sets and Multires are
still not taught — they are the later sculpting pieces, in
that order.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0: the flows battery (all six flows
walked end to end, sculpt_dyntopo included, plus its three
negative cases and the symmetry negatives), the interface
battery (including the coverage box), and the plain-layer
and search batteries. The 4.2.5 binary was not present on
the build machine this run, so the 4.2.5 pass is still owed;
the full parallel battery (test_tour) remains owed on an
unloaded machine, as recorded in the lane queue.
