# Blender Buddy v3.66.0 — animation's fifty-ninth piece: looking behind

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release.

**Make it look behind** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first fifty-eight animation
pieces — Buddy's fifty-ninth animation piece, teaching
looking behind, in four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will look behind** — one thing that will
   turn right around to face behind and come back.
2. **Remember its starting facing** — a facing
   memory at frame 1, level, rotation (0, 0, 0).
3. **Turn it right around to look behind, then bring it back** —
   turned facing (0, 0, 3.1416) remembered at frame 24, and
   the starting facing remembered again at frame 48, never
   leaning and in its own place the whole way.
4. **Watch it look behind, then save** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the facing memories
   in it.

The teaching point is that a look behind is the whole
half-circle turn and the coming back together: the
turn-around piece turned a thing the whole half circle
and left it facing the other way — a look behind turns
the whole half circle and returns, reading facings
(0, 0, 0) / (0, 0, 3.1416) / (0, 0, 0) at frames
1/24/48 in the reference build ((0, 0, 1.5708) partway back at frame 36). The checks sample the real evaluated facing
across the span: the span must run at least 20 frames,
the peak turn on the upright axis must land between 2.8 and 3.5, the end turn must settle within 0.35 of the starting facing, the
side axes must hold within 0.35, and the
thing must stay within 0.5 of its place. A probe on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5
before building showed the facing memories level at
frame 1, turned 3.1416 on the Z axis at frame 24 and
level again at frame 48 read back (span 47, peak
3.1416, end 0.0, side drift 0.0, place drift 0.0 in the probe); a turn around read peak 3.1416 with end 3.1416,
a spin read peak 6.2832 with end 6.2832, a wag read peak 0.4, a small look read peak 1.0 with end 1.0, a tilt read peak 0.0 with side drift 1.0, and a look that stayed
read end 3.1416 — all failing the bar. The probe ran on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 with byte-identical outputs.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a starting
facing alone does not prove the look behind, a turn around does not
prove it, a spin does not prove it, a wag does not prove it, a small look does not prove it, and a tilt does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 at the final
v3.66.0 source: the flows battery (all sixty-seven
flows plus the anim_lookback negatives), the interface
battery, and the plain-layer and search batteries all
pass on both versions. Proof picture: demo/anim_lookback_final.png — the
reference build's red thing at frame 24, fully turned
at (0, 0, 3.1416), looking behind in its own place.
