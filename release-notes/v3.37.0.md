# Blender Buddy v3.37.0 — animation's thirtieth piece: descending

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release.

**Make it descend** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first twenty-nine animation pieces —
Buddy's thirtieth animation piece, teaching descending, in
four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will descend** — one thing that will
   go down and forward together.
2. **Remember where it starts, up high** — a place
   memory at frame 1, its centre at least three units up.
3. **Descend it diagonally, down and forward in steps** — one
   unit forward and one unit down remembered at frame 17,
   another step remembered at frame 33, and the bottom —
   three units forward and three units down — remembered
   at frame 48.
4. **Watch it descend, then save it** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the place memories in it.

The teaching point is that a descend is the down and the
forward together: the fall sent a thing straight down, and
the slide sent it straight across the ground — a descend
does both at once, step by step, and never rises back
up, reading (1.4344, 0, 2.5656) partway down at frame 24
in the reference build's own places. The checks sample
the real evaluated place across the span: the span must
run at least 20 frames, the drop from the start height
to the end height must reach at least 2.5, the forward
travel between the ends must reach at least 2.5, and the
height must never rise back by more than 0.35 in a step.
A probe on Blender 5.3.0 and 5.2.0 before building (no
4.2.5 binary on this machine this run — that pass stays
owed) showed the place memories at (0, 0, 4), (1, 0, 3),
(2, 0, 2) and (3, 0, 1) across frames 1/17/33/48 read back
and interpolate identically on both versions (span 47,
drop 3.0, travel 3.0, height rises 0 in the probe); a fall
on the spot read travel 0.0, a slide read drop 0.0, a
climb read drop −3.0, a hop read drop 0.0, and a small
descend read drop 1.0 with travel 1.0 — all failing the bar.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a high starting place
alone does not prove the descend, a fall on its own place
does not prove it, a slide does not prove it, a climb does
not prove it, and a small descend does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 AND 5.2.0 at the final v3.37.0
source: the flows battery (all thirty-eight flows plus
the anim_descend negatives), the interface battery, and
the plain-layer and search batteries all pass on both
versions. The 4.2.5 pass stays owed — no 4.2.5 binary is
on this machine this run. Proof picture:
demo/anim_descend_final.png — the reference build's red
thing at frame 33, in the air partway down its descend.
