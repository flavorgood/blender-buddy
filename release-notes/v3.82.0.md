# Blender Buddy v3.82.0 — animation's seventy-fifth piece: wagging the other way

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release. The
public download still serves v3.20.0.

**Make it wag the other way** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first seventy-four animation
pieces — Buddy's seventy-fifth animation piece, teaching
wagging the other way, in four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will wag the other way** — one thing that will
   turn a little the other way first, and the other, again and again.
2. **Remember its starting facing** — a facing
   memory at frame 1, level, rotation (0, 0, 0).
3. **Wag it the other way and settle it forward** —
   rotation Z to -0.4 remembered at frame 13, +0.4 at frame 25,
   -0.4 again remembered at frame 37, back to (0, 0, 0)
   remembered at frame 48, the side axes
   staying level and the place held.
4. **Watch it wag the other way, then save it** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the facing memories
   in it.

The teaching point is that the direction a wag starts in is part of the wag:
the wag piece's checker read only the highest and lowest turns, so it could not tell the two starting directions apart — this piece's checker also records the sign of the first sampled turn past 0.2, the first extreme excursion, so the first way reads first_sign +1 while the other way reads first_sign -1 with the same high, low and reversals, reading facings
(0, 0, 0) / (0, 0, -0.4) / (0, 0, 0.4) / (0, 0, -0.4) / (0, 0, 0) at frames
1/13/25/37/48 in the reference build. The checks sample the real evaluated facing
across the span: the span must run at least 20 frames,
the lowest turn must land between -0.65 and -0.3, the highest turn must land between 0.3 and 0.65, the turn must change direction at least three times, the ends must land within 0.3 of the starting facing, the side axes must hold within 0.35, and the
thing must stay within 0.5 of its place, and the first extreme excursion must be negative. A probe on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5
before building showed the other-way reference read span 47, high 0.4, low -0.4, reversals 3, ends 0.0, side drift 0.0, place drift 0.0, first_sign -1; a first-way wag read the same high, low and reversals with first_sign +1,
a spin read high 6.2832 with ends 6.2832, a nod read high 0.0 with side drift 0.5, a sway read high 0.0 with side drift 0.8, a small wag the other way read high 0.15 and low -0.15 with first_sign 0, a single swing the other way read low -0.4 with reversals 1, and a turn-around the other way read low -3.1416 with ends 3.1416 — all failing the bar. The probe ran on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 with byte-identical outputs.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a starting
facing alone does not prove the wag the other way, a first-way wag does not
prove it, a spin does not prove it, a nod does not prove it, a sway does not prove it, a small wag the other way does not prove it, and a single swing the other way does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 at the final
v3.82.0 source: the flows battery (all eighty-three
flows plus the anim_otherwag negatives), the interface
battery, and the plain-layer and search batteries all
pass on both versions. Proof picture: demo/anim_otherwag_final.png — the
reference build's red thing at frame 13, turned the other way first
at facing (0, 0, -0.4) in its own place.
