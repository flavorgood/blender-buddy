# Blender Buddy v3.31.0 — animation's twenty-fourth piece: pacing

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release.

**Make it pace** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first twenty-three animation pieces —
Buddy's twenty-fourth animation piece, teaching pacing, in
four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will pace** — one thing that will
   walk its ground out and back.
2. **Remember where it starts, its home place** — a place
   memory at frame 1, the thing resting on the ground where
   it will return.
3. **Send it far out and bring it home** — one long trip
   out across the ground, the far place remembered in the
   middle, home remembered again at the last frame.
4. **Watch it pace, then save it** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the place memories in it.

The teaching point is that a pace is a journey with a
return: the slide went somewhere and stayed there, a pace
comes back — reading (3, 0, 1) at its far place at frame
24 and (0, 0, 1) back home at frame 48. The checks sample
the real evaluated place across the span: the span must
run at least 20 frames, the travel on the busiest sideways
axis at least 2.5 units, the movement must turn around at
least once, the end must land within 0.3 of the start, and
the height must hold within 0.35 the whole way across. A
probe on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 before building showed
the place memories at (0, 0, 1), (3, 0, 1) and (0, 0, 1)
across frames 1/24/48 read back and interpolate identically
on both versions (span 47, range 3.0, reversals 1, ends
gap 0.0, height variation 0.0 in the probe); a one-way
slide read reversals 0 with ends gap 3.0, a bounce read
range 0.0 with height variation 2.0, a small back-and-forth
read range 0.5, and a shake read range 1.4 — all failing
the bar.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a home place alone
does not prove the pace, a one-way slide does not prove
it, a small back-and-forth does not prove it, a bounce
does not prove it, and a shake does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 AND 4.2.5 at the final v3.31.0
source: the flows battery (all thirty-two flows plus
the anim_pace negatives), the interface battery, and
the plain-layer and search batteries all pass on both
versions. Proof picture: demo/anim_pace_final.png —
the reference build's red thing at frame 24, at its far
place, the blue home pad on the ground where it started
and will return.
