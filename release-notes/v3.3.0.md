# Blender Buddy v3.3.0 — sculpting's first piece

**Sculpt it like clay**: a new guided way of working under "Learn by doing", Buddy's first sculpting coverage — built and proven separately, the way the Donut proved modeling.

Six steps, in the sculptor's order:

1. **Start with a ball of clay** — one even shape with enough surface to push around (the monkey works too).
2. **Pick up the brushes** — Sculpt Mode; F is brush size, Shift+F is strength; Draw, Grab, and Smooth (Shift) are the brushes the flow teaches.
3. **Block the big forms** — big brush, big movements; the whole form decided while the clay is coarse.
4. **Give it more clay (remesh)** — Ctrl+R rebuilds the surface even and finer, only once the forms read.
5. **Work the finer detail** — small brush, light passes, Smooth to take roughness back off.
6. **Smooth finish, then save the clay** — shade smooth, save the source file.

Every step ticks only when the surface itself proves it. A perfect ball has every point the same distance from its centre, so blocked forms are read from the spread of those distances; fine detail is read by how much of the surface a smoothed copy of it has to erase. A plain untouched ball ticks only step 1 — that negative case is tested alongside the full walk.

The Start screen's coverage box now says sculpting has a first guided piece — and that it is a first piece, not the whole craft. Animation and the other crafts remain untaught, and still say so.

Verified: flows battery (all four flows walked end to end) and interface battery on Blender 5.3.0 and Blender 4.2.5 LTS; plain-layer, context and search batteries on 5.3.0. The full parallel battery's longest suite (test_tour) timed out on the build machine at this version and at the v3.2.7 baseline alike — recorded in the lane queue, not claimed as a pass.
