# Blender Buddy v3.74.0 — animation's sixty-seventh piece: turning around the other way

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release. The
public download still serves v3.20.0.

**Make it turn around the other way** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first sixty-six animation
pieces — Buddy's sixty-seventh animation piece, teaching
turning around the other way, in four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will turn around the other way** — one thing that will
   turn a half turn the other way and stay facing behind it.
2. **Remember its starting facing** — a facing
   memory at frame 1, level, rotation (0, 0, 0).
3. **Turn it a half turn the other way and keep it turned** —
   rotation Z to -3.1416 remembered at frame 24, and
   the same turned facing, rotation Z to -3.1416,
   remembered again at frame 48,
   the side axes staying level and the place held the whole way.
4. **Watch it turn around the other way, then save** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the facing memories
   in it.

The teaching point is that the other way is the opposite half turn:
the turn-around piece turned a thing half a turn one way — this piece turns
it half a turn the other way, reading facings
(0, 0, 0) / (0, 0, -3.1416) / (0, 0, -3.1416) at frames
1/24/48 in the reference build ((0, 0, -3.1416) at frame 36 as well). The checks sample the real evaluated facing
across the span: the span must run at least 20 frames,
the end turn on the upright axis must land between -3.45 and -2.8, the side axes must hold within 0.35, and the
thing must stay within 0.5 of its place. A probe on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5
before building showed the facing memories
level at frame 1, turned -3.1416 on the Z axis at frame 24 and -3.1416 again at frame 48 read back (span 47, ends
-3.1416, side drift 0.0, place drift 0.0 in the probe); a turn around the first way read ends 3.1416,
a spin the other way read ends -6.2832, a spin the first way read ends 6.2832, a wag read ends 0.0, a small turn read ends -0.4, a tilt read ends 0.0 with side drift 1.0, and a turn that returned
read ends 0.0 — all failing the bar. The probe ran on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 with byte-identical outputs.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a starting
facing alone does not prove the turn around the other way, a turn around the first way does not
prove it, a spin the other way does not prove it, a spin the first way does not prove it, a wag does not prove it, a small turn does not prove it, a tilt does not prove it, and a turn that returns does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 at the final
v3.74.0 source: the flows battery (all seventy-five
flows plus the anim_otherturn negatives), the interface
battery, and the plain-layer and search batteries all
pass on both versions. Proof picture: demo/anim_otherturn_final.png — the
reference build's red thing at frame 24, turned around the other way
at facing (0, 0, -3.1416) in its own place (a cube reads the same turned around, so the numeric proof carries it, as with the turn pieces).
