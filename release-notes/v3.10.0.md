# Blender Buddy v3.10.0 — animation's third piece: cameras

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes.

**Move the camera** sits under "Learn by doing" beside
the modeling, sculpting and first two animation pieces
— Buddy's third animation piece, teaching the
travelling shot, in five steps:

1. **Set up the shot** — a subject, and the scene's
   camera placed a few paces away and aimed at it.
2. **Remember the camera's first place** — the camera's
   place AND facing remembered at frame 1, the way any
   thing's start is remembered.
3. **Move the camera, and remember again** — a clearly
   different place at frame 48, and Blender fills in
   the travelling shot between the two memories.
4. **Aim it at the subject again** — the facing
   remembered at BOTH ends of the move, so the camera
   turns through the journey and the subject stays in
   frame the whole way.
5. **Watch the move, then save it** — watched through
   the camera itself, and the file saved with the
   camera's memories still on it.

The teaching point is the one the first piece taught,
moved to the camera: in Blender a camera is a thing —
it has a place, a facing, and it carries keyframes
exactly the way a mover does. The checks read the
camera's own memories and its real travel the same
way "Make it move" reads a thing's. The aim step is
checked from the camera's real facing: the play line
is put on the first and last memories, the camera's
look direction is read from its remembered facing,
and the angle to the subject must be within 0.30
radians at both ends. A probe on Blender 4.2.5 before
building showed why the bar separates the work: aimed
at the subject the angle reads 0.0, while a camera
moved to the reference build's second place carrying
its old facing misses the subject by 1.36 radians —
a moved camera that was never re-aimed cannot tick
the step for free.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1 (it already has a
camera and a cube — the shot's raw material, nothing
remembered), one starting memory alone does not prove
the camera move, and a camera moved without its
facing remembered at the new place does not prove
the aim.

The Start screen's coverage box now names all three
animation pieces — "Make it move", "Make it glide"
and "Move the camera" — and still says plainly that
they are first pieces of the craft, not the whole of
it; characters remain a later piece, and the other
crafts remain untaught.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5: the flows
battery (all eleven flows walked end to end,
anim_camera included, plus its three negative cases
and the anim_move, anim_ease and sculpting
negatives), the interface battery (including the
coverage box), and the plain-layer and search
batteries. The full parallel battery (test_tour)
remains owed on an unloaded machine, as recorded in
the lane queue.
