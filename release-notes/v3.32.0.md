# Blender Buddy v3.32.0 — animation's twenty-fifth piece: tilting

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release.

**Make it tilt** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first twenty-four animation pieces —
Buddy's twenty-fifth animation piece, teaching tilting, in
four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will tilt** — one thing that will
   lean over to one side.
2. **Remember its starting facing, upright** — a facing
   memory at frame 1, level on every axis.
3. **Lean it over and remember the lean** — leaned a real
   lean, short of going over, remembered at the last frame.
4. **Watch it tilt, then save it** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the facing memories in it.

The teaching point is that a tilt is a lean, not a
tumble: the spin turned a thing around where it stood
and the flip turned it all the way over, a tilt does
neither — reading 0.484 partway leaned at frame 24 and
1.0 leaned at frame 48. The checks sample the real
evaluated facing across the span: the span must run at
least 20 frames, the lean at the end must reach at least
0.6 radians on a side axis but no more than 1.8, the
lean must never pass 2.2 anywhere across the span, the
upright facing must hold within 0.35, and the thing must
stay within 0.5 of its place. A probe on Blender 5.3.0
and 4.2.5 before building showed the facing memories
level at frame 1 and leaned 1.0 on the X axis at frame
48 read back and interpolate identically on both
versions (span 47, end tilt 1.0, peak 1.0, upright
drift 0.0 in the probe); a spin read end tilt 0.0 with
upright drift 6.2832, a flip read end tilt 6.2832, a
small lean read 0.25, a rock that settles upright read
end tilt 0.0, and an over-lean read 2.6 — all failing
the bar.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, an upright facing alone
does not prove the tilt, a spin does not prove it, a flip
does not prove it, and a small lean does not prove it
either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 AND 4.2.5 at the final v3.32.0
source: the flows battery (all thirty-three flows plus
the anim_tilt negatives), the interface battery, and
the plain-layer and search batteries all pass on both
versions. Proof picture: demo/anim_tilt_final.png —
the reference build's red thing at frame 48, leaned
over to one side at 1.0 radian and staying leaned, on
the ground.
