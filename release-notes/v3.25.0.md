# Blender Buddy v3.25.0 — animation's eighteenth piece: falling

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release.

**Make it fall** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first seventeen animation pieces —
Buddy's eighteenth animation piece, teaching falling, in
four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will fall** — one thing that will
   drop.
2. **Remember where it starts, up high** — a place memory
   at frame 1, its centre at least three units up, resting
   in the air.
3. **Let it drop to the ground and stay there** — the
   landing remembered at frame 48 at ground level, its
   centre one unit up for the usual two-unit thing.
4. **Watch it fall, then save it** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the place memories in it.

The teaching point is that a fall is a one-way trip, the
mirror of the bounce: the bounce came back, a fall does
not — at frame 24 the thing reads (0, 0, 2.5479), partway
down between its high start and the ground. The checks
read the real evaluated place at the first and last
memories: the span must run at least 20 frames, the drop
at least 1.5 units, the end height at most 1.3 so the
thing truly lands, and the sideways drift at most half
the drop, so a fall goes down, not sideways. A probe on
Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 before building showed the place
memories across frames 1/48 read back and interpolate
identically on both versions (span 47, drop 3.0, end
height 1.0, drift 0.0 in the probe; the shipped check
reads the same); a slide at height read drop 0.0, a
down-then-up bounce read drop 0.0 and end height 4.0, a
small dip read drop 0.5, and a fall drifting 2.0 sideways
over a 3.0 drop read drift 2.0 — all failing the bars.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a high start alone does
not prove the fall, a sideways slide does not prove it,
and a small dip (0.5 of drop) does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 AND 4.2.5 at the final v3.25.0
source: the flows battery (all twenty-six flows plus
the anim_fall negatives), the interface battery, and
the plain-layer and search batteries all pass on both
versions. Proof picture: demo/anim_fall_final.png —
the reference build's red thing at frame 24, partway
down between its high start and the ground, with its
shadow below.
