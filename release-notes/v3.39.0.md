# Blender Buddy v3.39.0 — animation's thirty-second piece: landing

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release.

**Make it land** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first thirty-one animation pieces —
Buddy's thirty-second animation piece, teaching landing, in
four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will land** — one thing that will
   come down and stay down.
2. **Remember where it starts, up high** — a place
   memory at frame 1, its centre at least 3 units up.
3. **Bring it down and keep it there** — three units down
   remembered at frame 24, and the same ground place
   remembered again at frame 48, in its own place the
   whole way.
4. **Watch it land, then save it** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the place memories in it.

The teaching point is that a landing is the drop and the
staying together: the fall sent a thing down and only
arrived at the end of its span — a landing arrives by the
middle and holds its ground place to the end, reading
(0, 0, 2.5978) partway down at frame 12 in the reference
build's own places. The checks sample the real evaluated
place across the span: the span must run at least 20
frames, the drop from the start height to the end height
must reach at least 2.5, the end height must be no more
than 1.3, the highest height from the middle of the span
onward must stay no more than 1.7, and no sampled place may
stray more than 0.5 sideways from the start place. A
probe on Blender 4.2.5 before building showed the place
memories at (0, 0, 4), (0, 0, 1) and (0, 0, 1) across
frames 1/24/48 read back as span 47, drop 3.0, end 1.0,
ceiling 1.0, drift 0.0 in the probe; a plain fall read
ceiling 2.5479, a bounce read end 4.0 with ceiling 4.0, a
slide at height read drop 0.0 with drift 3.0, a small drop
read drop 1.0, and a diagonal descend read ceiling 2.5
with drift 3.0 — all failing the bar. No Blender 5.3.0
binary is on this cell this run, so the 5.3.0 battery pass
for this piece is owed (the descend piece had the same
gap on its run and it was discharged on the next run).

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a high starting place
alone does not prove the landing, a plain fall does not
prove it, a bounce does not prove it, a slide at height
does not prove it, and a small drop does not prove it
either.

Verified on Blender 4.2.5 at the final v3.39.0 source:
the flows battery (all forty flows plus the anim_land
negatives), the interface battery, and the plain-layer
and search batteries all pass. Proof picture:
demo/anim_land_final.png — the reference build's red
thing at frame 36, landed on the ground in its own place
with its shadow below.
