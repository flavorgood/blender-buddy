# Blender Buddy v3.83.0 — animation's seventy-sixth piece: swaying the other way

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release. The
public download still serves v3.20.0.

**Make it sway the other way** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first seventy-five animation
pieces — Buddy's seventy-sixth animation piece, teaching
swaying the other way, in four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will sway the other way** — one thing that will
   lean from side to side, the other side first.
2. **Remember its starting facing, upright** — a facing
   memory at frame 1, level, rotation (0, 0, 0).
3. **Sway it side to side the other way and settle it upright** —
   rotation X to -0.8 remembered at frame 17, +0.8 at frame 33,
   back to (0, 0, 0)
   remembered at frame 48, the side axes
   staying level and the place held.
4. **Watch it sway the other way, then save it** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the facing memories
   in it.

The teaching point is that the direction a sway starts in is part of the sway:
the sway piece's checker read only the most and least leans, so it could not tell the two starting directions apart — this piece's checker also records the sign of the first sampled lean past 0.2, the first extreme excursion, so the first way reads first_sign +1 while the other way reads first_sign -1 with the same most, least and reversals, reading facings
(0, 0, 0) / (-0.8, 0, 0) / (0.8, 0, 0) / (0, 0, 0) at frames
1/17/33/48 in the reference build. The checks sample the real evaluated facing
across the span: the span must run at least 20 frames,
the most lean must reach at least +0.6, the least lean must reach at least -0.6, the lean must change direction at least twice, the ends must land within 0.3 of level, the upright axis must hold within 0.35, and the
thing must stay within 0.5 of its place, and the first extreme excursion must be negative. A probe on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5
before building showed the other-way reference read span 47, most 0.8, least -0.8, reversals 2, ends 0.0, upright drift 0.0, place drift 0.0, first_sign -1; a first-way sway read the same most, least and reversals with first_sign +1,
a tilt read most 0.0 with least -0.8, reversals 0 and ends 0.8, a spin read upright drift 6.2832 with first_sign 0, a small sway the other way read most 0.2 and least -0.2, a one-way rock the other way read most 0.0 with least -0.8 and reversals 1, and a bow read most 0.0 with least -0.9 and reversals 1 — all failing the bar. The probe ran on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 with byte-identical outputs.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a starting
facing alone does not prove the sway the other way, a first-way sway does not
prove it, a tilt does not prove it, a spin does not prove it, a small sway the other way does not prove it, a one-way rock the other way does not prove it, and a bow does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 at the final
v3.83.0 source: the flows battery (all eighty-four
flows plus the anim_othersway negatives), the interface
battery, and the plain-layer and search batteries all
pass on both versions. Proof picture: demo/anim_othersway_final.png — the
reference build's red thing at frame 17, leaned the other way first
at facing (-0.8, 0, 0) in its own place.
