# Blender Buddy v3.38.0 — animation's thirty-first piece: hovering

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release.

**Make it hover** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first thirty animation pieces —
Buddy's thirty-first animation piece, teaching hovering, in
four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will hover** — one thing that will
   rise and stay up.
2. **Remember where it starts, on the ground** — a place
   memory at frame 1, its centre no more than 1.3 units up.
3. **Rise it up high and keep it there** — three units up
   remembered at frame 24, and the same high place
   remembered again at frame 48, in its own place the
   whole way.
4. **Watch it hover, then save it** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the place memories in it.

The teaching point is that a hover is the rise and the
staying together: the rise sent a thing up and only
arrived at the end of its span — a hover arrives by the
middle and holds its high place to the end, reading
(0, 0, 2.4022) partway up at frame 12 in the reference
build's own places. The checks sample the real evaluated
place across the span: the span must run at least 20
frames, the rise from the start height to the end height
must reach at least 2.5, the end height must reach at
least 3.0, the lowest height from the middle of the span
onward must stay at least 2.8, and no sampled place may
stray more than 0.5 sideways from the start place. A
probe on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 before building showed
the place memories at (0, 0, 1), (0, 0, 4) and (0, 0, 4)
across frames 1/24/48 read back and interpolate
identically on both versions (span 47, rise 3.0, end
4.0, hold 4.0, drift 0.0 in the probe); a plain rise read
hold 2.4521, a bounce read end 1.0 with hold 1.0, a slide
read rise 0.0 with drift 3.0, a small lift read rise 1.0
with end 2.0, and a diagonal climb read hold 2.5 with
drift 3.0 — all failing the bar.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a ground starting place
alone does not prove the hover, a plain rise does not
prove it, a bounce does not prove it, a slide does not
prove it, and a small lift does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 AND 4.2.5 at the final v3.38.0
source: the flows battery (all thirty-nine flows plus
the anim_hover negatives), the interface battery, and
the plain-layer and search batteries all pass on both
versions. Proof picture: demo/anim_hover_final.png —
the reference build's red thing at frame 36, hovering
high in its own place with its shadow below on the
ground.
