# Blender Buddy v3.34.0 — animation's twenty-seventh piece: hopping

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release.

**Make it hop** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first twenty-six animation pieces —
Buddy's twenty-seventh animation piece, teaching hopping, in
four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will hop** — one thing that will
   leave the ground and land somewhere else.
2. **Remember where it starts, on the ground** — a place
   memory at frame 1.
3. **Hop it up and across, and land it far away** — up two
   units and halfway across remembered at frame 24, landed
   three units across at frame 48, back at its starting
   height.
4. **Watch it hop, then save it** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the place memories in it.

The teaching point is that a hop is the arc and the journey
together: the bounce sent a thing up and brought it back
down in its own place, and the slide sent it across the
ground without ever leaving it — a hop does both at once,
reading (0.4381, 0, 0.9348) partway up at frame 12 and
(1.5, 0, 2.0) up in the air halfway across at frame 24 in
the reference build's own places. The checks sample the
real evaluated place across the span: the span must run at
least 20 frames, the height must clear at least 1.5 above
the lower end somewhere across the span, the landing must
sit at least 2.0 sideways from the start, and the two ends
must sit within 0.3 of each other in height. A probe on
Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 before building showed the place
memories at (0, 0, 1), (1.5, 0, 3) and (3, 0, 1) across
frames 1/24/48 read back and interpolate identically on
both versions (span 47, peak 2.0, travel 3.0, ends gap 0.0
in the probe); a bounce in place read travel 0.0, a slide
read peak 0.0, a travelling rise read ends 3.0, and a
small hop read peak 0.5 with travel 1.0 — all failing
the bar.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a starting place alone
does not prove the hop, a bounce in its own place does not
prove it, a slide does not prove it, and a small hop does
not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 AND 4.2.5 at the final v3.34.0
source: the flows battery (all thirty-five flows plus
the anim_hop negatives), the interface battery, and
the plain-layer and search batteries all pass on both
versions. Proof picture: demo/anim_hop_final.png —
the reference build's red thing at frame 24, up in the
air halfway across its hop, its shadow directly below
on the ground.
