# Blender Buddy v3.35.0 — animation's twenty-eighth piece: zigzagging

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release.

**Make it zigzag** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first twenty-seven animation pieces —
Buddy's twenty-eighth animation piece, teaching zigzagging, in
four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will zigzag** — one thing that will
   travel forward turning side to side.
2. **Remember where it starts, on the ground** — a place
   memory at frame 1.
3. **Zigzag it forward, turning side to side** — out to one
   side remembered at frame 13, back across the line at
   frame 25, out to the side again at frame 37, and the far
   end remembered at frame 48, on the ground the whole way.
4. **Watch it zigzag, then save it** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the place memories in it.

The teaching point is that a zigzag is the forward journey
and the side-to-side turns together: the slide sent a thing
straight across the ground, and the pace sent it out and
brought it home — a zigzag keeps travelling forward while
it turns, reading (1.0679, 1.4705) near the first turn at
frame 12 and (3.6, 1.5) out at its last turn at frame 37 in
the reference build's own places. The checks sample the
real evaluated place across the span: the span must run at
least 20 frames, the forward travel between the ends must
reach at least 2.5, the sideways range must reach at least
1.0, the sideways direction must turn at least twice, and
the height must hold within 0.35. A probe on Blender 5.3.0
and 4.2.5 before building showed the place memories at
(0, 0, 1), (1.2, 1.5, 1), (2.4, 0, 1), (3.6, 1.5, 1) and
(4.8, 0, 1) across frames 1/13/25/37/48 read back and
interpolate identically on both versions (span 47, forward
travel 4.8, sideways range 1.5, reversals 3, height
variation 0.0 in the probe); a slide read reversals 0, a
pace read forward travel 0.0, a small zigzag read forward
travel 1.2 with reversals 1, and a hop read height
variation 2.0 — all failing the bar.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a starting place alone
does not prove the zigzag, a straight slide does not
prove it, a small zigzag does not prove it, and a hop
does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 AND 4.2.5 at the final v3.35.0
source: the flows battery (all thirty-six flows plus
the anim_zigzag negatives), the interface battery, and
the plain-layer and search batteries all pass on both
versions. Proof picture: demo/anim_zigzag_final.png —
the reference build's red thing at frame 37, out at its
last turn, on the ground.
