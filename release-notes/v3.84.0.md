# Blender Buddy v3.84.0 — animation's seventy-seventh piece: swinging the other way

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release. The
public download still serves v3.20.0.

**Make it swing the other way** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first seventy-six animation
pieces — Buddy's seventy-seventh animation piece, teaching
swinging the other way, in four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will swing the other way** — one thing that will
   lean both ways, the other way first.
2. **Remember its starting facing, leaned the other way** — a facing
   memory at frame 1, leaned the other way, rotation Y about +0.7.
3. **Lean it the first way, and back** —
   rotation Y to -0.7 remembered at frame 24,
   back to the first lean
   remembered at frame 48.
4. **Watch it swing the other way, then save it** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the facing memories
   in it.

The teaching point is that the lean a swing starts from is part of the swing:
the swing piece's arc checker read only the span, the lean range, the ends gap and the lowest and highest leans on the busiest facing axis, so it is symmetric and could not tell the two ways apart — this piece's measure also records the starting lean itself, the evaluated facing on that busiest axis at the first remembered frame, the absolute leaned value, so the first way reads starting lean -0.7 while the other way reads starting lean +0.7 with the same span, range, ends, low and high, reading facings
(0, 0.7, 0) / (0, -0.7, 0) / (0, 0.7, 0) at frames
1/24/48 in the reference build. The checks sample the real evaluated facing
across the span: the span must run at least 20 frames,
the lean must sweep at least one radian end to end, the lowest lean must reach at least -0.35, the highest lean must reach at least +0.35, the ends must land within 0.3 of each other, at least three facing memories must be held, and the starting lean must reach at least +0.3, leaned the other way. A probe on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5
before building showed the other-way reference read span 47, range 1.4, ends 0.0, low -0.7, high 0.7, starting lean 0.7; a first-way swing read the same span, range, ends, low and high with starting lean -0.7,
a tip that never crosses the middle read range 0.7 with low 0.7 and high 1.4, a small swing the other way read range 0.4 with starting lean 0.2, a one-way move held only 2 memories, and a swing starting upright read starting lean 0.0 with high 0.0 — all failing the bar. The probe ran on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 with byte-identical outputs.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a starting
lean the other way alone does not prove the swing the other way, a first-way swing does not
prove it, a tip that never crosses the middle does not prove it, a small swing the other way does not prove it, a one-way move does not prove it, and a swing starting upright does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 at the final
v3.84.0 source: the flows battery (all eighty-five
flows plus the anim_otherswing negatives), the interface
battery, and the plain-layer and search batteries all
pass on both versions. Proof picture: demo/anim_otherswing_final.png — the
reference build's red thing at frame 24, leaned the first way
at facing (0, -0.7, 0) mid-swing, in its own place.
