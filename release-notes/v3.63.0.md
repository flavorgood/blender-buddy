# Blender Buddy v3.63.0 — animation's fifty-sixth piece: turning to the side

One new guided flow, and nothing else changes. Built and
held locally under the owner's 2026-11-26 release-batching
rule — this is batch bookkeeping, not a separate release;
it ships together with the first batched release.

**Make it turn to the side** sits under "Learn by doing" beside the
modeling, sculpting and first fifty-five animation
pieces — Buddy's fifty-sixth animation piece, teaching
turning to the side, in four steps:

1. **Pick the thing that will turn to the side** — one thing that will
   turn a quarter turn and face sideways.
2. **Remember its starting facing** — a facing
   memory at frame 1, level, i.e. rotation Z 0.
3. **Turn it a quarter turn to the side and keep it turned** —
   rotation Z to 1.5708 remembered at frame 24, and
   the same turned facing remembered again at frame 48, side axes
   staying level and place held the whole way.
4. **Watch it turn to the side, then save** — played across the
   span, and the file saved with the facing memories
   in it.

The teaching point is that a turn to the side is the quarter
turn and the staying turned together: the turn-around piece
turned a thing the whole half circle — a turn to the side
turns only a quarter turn, keeps it turned, and leaves it facing
sideways, reading facing Z
0 / 1.5708 / 1.5708 at frames
1/24/48 in the reference build (1.5708 again at frame 36). The checks sample the real evaluated facing
across the span: the span must run at least 20 frames,
the end turn on the upright axis must land between 1.25 and 1.9, the side axes must hold within 0.35, and the
thing must stay within 0.5 of its place. A probe on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5
before building showed the facing memories level at
frame 1 and turned 1.5708 on the Z axis at frames
24 and 48 read back (span 47, ends 1.5708, side drift
0.0, place drift 0.0 in the probe); a turn around read ends 3.1416,
a spin read ends 6.2832, a wag read ends 0.0, a small turn read ends 0.4, a tilt read ends 0.0 with side drift 1.0, and a turn that returned
read ends 0.0 — all failing the bar. The probe ran on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 with byte-identical outputs.

Negative cases are in the battery alongside the walk:
a plain scene ticks only step 1, a starting
facing alone does not prove the turn to the side, a turn around does not
prove it, a spin does not prove it, a wag does not prove it, a small turn does not prove it, a tilt does not
prove it, and a turn that returns does not prove it either.

Verified on Blender 5.3.0 and 4.2.5 at the final
v3.63.0 source: the flows battery (all sixty-four
flows plus the anim_sideturn negatives), the interface
battery, and the plain-layer and search batteries all
pass on both versions. Proof picture: demo/anim_sideturn_final.png — the
reference build's red thing at frame 36, turned a
quarter turn to the side and facing sideways.
